## Problem

There is no checkpoint between "proposal accepted" and "WI promoted-as-actionable." The Codex burst of 2026-05-10..12 promoted 11 WIs in 2 days; review (`docs/specs/reviews/codex-recent-wi-review-2026-05-12.md`) traced 8 of 14 findings to this single structural gap. The framework has `plan-changeset`, `concerns/`, `rules/tier-1-promotion.md`, the severity taxonomy in `rules/github-projects.md`, and the host-agnostic principle in `CLAUDE.md` — but no skill orchestrates them at the WI-promotion moment.
