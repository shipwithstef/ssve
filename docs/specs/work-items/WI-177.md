# WI-177: 13 legacy knowledge domains remain unproven

## Problem

The framework reliability review found HIGH issue F-007: 13 legacy knowledge domains remain unproven.

## Evidence

The same provenance validator warns on `namecheap`, `agent-harnesses`, `mimo`, `gemini-cli-hooks`, `gcp-mcp`, `google-ai-pro`, `agent-evals`, `resend`, `capacitor`, `devops-mcp`, `claude-hooks`, `claude-design`, and `codex`.

## Impact

Old unproven knowledge can still drive design decisions while only newer slices are gated.

## Proposed Fix

Create a backfill queue with owners, dates, and retirement rules. Mark unbackfilled domains as "legacy-unverified" in recall output.

## Closeout

Closed 2026-05-11. The 13 legacy-unverified knowledge domains are now tracked in
`references/knowledge/domains/legacy-backfill-queue.json` with owner, due date,
status, action, and retirement rules. `recall-stack-knowledge` now labels served
unproven slices as `legacy-unverified` and ties recall output back to the queue
until each domain is backfilled or retired.
