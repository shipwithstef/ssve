## Audit Verdict — INVALID (close, do not ship)

The 12 coreyhaines skills are **external-by-design**, not missing from the canonical registry. Verified:
- `skills-manifest.json` registers them under `externalAddOns.coreyhaines-marketing-pack` (separate repo `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`, pinned commit `1bcff9fc`)
- `EXTERNAL_ADDONS.md` § "Add-On: coreyhaines marketing ecosystem (optional, v1.9.0)" documents them as a separately-cloned pack
- The split between `includedSkills` (svc-native) and `externalAddOns` (optional packs) is intentional — adding to `includedSkills` would force forking the upstream repo

The proposed fix would damage architecture. Routing to these skills works correctly when the addon is installed via setup; the manifest's two-registry design is the documented mechanism.

**Action for Kimi:** do nothing — this WI is closed. Optional follow-up: add a 1-line schema comment in `skills-manifest.json` clarifying that `externalAddOns` skills don't appear in `includedSkills` by design (file as MEDIUM if desired, separate WI).

## Problem
`skills-manifest.json` `externalAddOns.coreyhaines-marketing-pack` lists 12 skills:
`product-marketing-context`, `customer-research`, `market-competitors`, `competitor-alternatives`, `copywriting`, `page-cro`, `launch-strategy`, `market-social`, `market-ads`, `market-emails`, `signup-flow-cro`, `onboarding-cro`.

**All 12 are missing from `includedSkills`.**

## Impact
The framework claims these skills are available as external add-ons, but they don't exist in the canonical registry. A user routing to them gets "no such skill." External addon routing is completely broken.
