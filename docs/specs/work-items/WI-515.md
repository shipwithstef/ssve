# WI-515: Drain the proposal triage backlog and stop it silently re-accumulating

**Type:** framework hygiene
**Status:** identified
**Severity:** medium
**Filed:** 2026-08-04
**Source:** WI-512 landing hygiene — measured during the batch; the counts the source plan carried did not survive measurement
**Lane:** framework

## Problem

`proposals/triage.json` declares `max_open_days: 1` and `max_defer_days: 14`. Measured against those declared limits on 2026-08-04:

| Measure | Count | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Open proposal `.md` files (excluding `.template.md`) | 43 | `proposals/` |
| Archived proposals | 253 | `proposals/done/` |
| Triage entries | 70 (67 before the WI-512 hygiene commit added 3) | `triage.json` `entries` |
| Entries with an **expired** `deferred_until` | 17 | as of 2026-08-04 |
| Open proposals with **no triage entry at all** | 25 → 22 after WI-512 | 43 open files |

WI-512's landing hygiene triaged the three proposals it was directly acting on. **22 open proposals remain untriaged and 17 deferrals remain expired** — that is the actual backlog, and it is filed here rather than smuggled into an unrelated batch.

The plan that WI-512 executed asserted "26 expired triage deferrals, 3 untracked proposals". Neither figure reproduces. Recording the discrepancy is the point: this is the §3g rule-1 failure mode (a claim stated without its denominator) occurring inside the very plan that landed §3g.

## Scope

### In scope

- Disposition each of the 22 untriaged open proposals: accept-and-promote to a WI, defer with a date and a reason, or archive to `proposals/done/`.
- Re-disposition the 17 expired deferrals — a deferral that has expired is a decision that was never made, not a decision to keep waiting.
- Determine why untriaged proposals accumulate at all: `max_open_days: 1` means a proposal should be triaged within a day of landing, so 22 untriaged files is not drift, it is an unenforced policy.

### Out of scope

- Re-litigating the technical merit of any individual proposal beyond what its disposition requires.
- Changing `max_open_days` / `max_defer_days` without evidence about what a workable cadence actually is.

## The load-bearing question

A hygiene pass that drains the backlog without answering **why nothing enforced `max_open_days: 1`** buys one clean snapshot and nothing else — the backlog simply re-accumulates, which is demonstrably what has happened. Prefer a mechanical check (a tier-1 validator over `proposals/` vs `triage.json`, reporting untriaged files and expired deferrals with counts) over a one-time manual sweep. If the drain is done manually, the enforcement gap must still be filed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC-1** Every open proposal in `proposals/` has a triage entry, or is archived to `proposals/done/`.
- [ ] **AC-2** No entry carries a `deferred_until` in the past.
- [ ] **AC-3** A mechanical check reports untriaged proposals and expired deferrals with explicit counts and denominators, so the next occurrence is detected rather than discovered.
- [ ] **AC-4** Whatever cadence the check enforces matches the declared `max_open_days` / `max_defer_days`, or those values are changed with the evidence for the new numbers recorded.
- [ ] **AC-5** Proposals promoted to WIs carry the WI id in their triage entry, so the accept path is auditable rather than implied.
