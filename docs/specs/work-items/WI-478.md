## Problem

The ad fleet (ad-strategist → ad-video-script → ad-video-producer) is video-first and its placement heuristics predate Meta's 2025 Andromeda change. Upstream `ads` 2.1.0 documents the shift: audience knowledge goes into creative, not targeting filters (Meta 80%+ creative; Google Search still 60% targeting; per-platform split table); statics + volume often beat polished video on Meta; interest-stacking is now actively harmful. Operating on stale priors burns real ad spend before the campaigns ledger learns it.
