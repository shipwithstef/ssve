# WI-491: Bounded adversarial review loop (3-round cap + high-disposition)

**Type:** bugfix
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Severity:** high
**Filed:** 2026-07-16
**Source:** owner directive after WI-486's plan review ran 9 rounds without converging
**Lane:** framework
**Related:** WI-486, WI-489

---

## Problem (root cause)

The adversarial review loop had no bounded exit for **persistent High findings**.
`review-cross-model/SKILL.md` said "repeat until no Critical, no High, only Medium
remain" but its cap only escalated on persistent **Critical** — so a High-only
stream (which an adversarial reviewer always produces on a genuinely complex plan)
had NO terminal condition and looped indefinitely. WI-486's plan review ran **9
rounds** (rubric flat at 3, 0 Critical, ~6 persistent High) before the loop was
manually capped. `references/plan-review-protocol.md` had the correct
disposition-based terminal condition, but the two documents conflicted and neither
was mechanically enforced.

## Fix

- `review-cross-model/SKILL.md` §Convergence Loop: HARD 3-round cap; convergence is
  by DISPOSITION not by the reviewer running dry. After round 3: unresolved Critical
  → escalate (block); High-only → disposition each remaining High
  (`accept-with-justification` as a logged execution-time risk, or
  `reject-with-justification`) and TERMINATE. Never a round 4 for High-only.
- `references/plan-review-protocol.md`: same bounded terminal condition made canonical.
- `scripts/check-review-round-cap.mjs`: mechanical enforcement — fails an unbounded
  (>3 round, undispositioned) loop; passes a bounded exit or ≤3 rounds.
- `test-framework/evals/tier-1/validate-review-round-cap.sh`: tier-1 guard.
