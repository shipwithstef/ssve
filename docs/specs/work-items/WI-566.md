## Problem

WI-491 requires a review to stop after three rounds and proceed by disposition
when zero Critical findings remain. Schema-v3 reviewer evidence rejects the same
terminal state whenever the immutable external reviewer still says raw `fail`.

## Root cause

The round-cap checker and skill doctrine gained a bounded local adjudication,
but the receipt contract retained the external raw verdict as its only
promotion primitive. No candidate-bound representation connected the two.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: Plan and execution receipts accept a terminal raw `fail` only with a schema-valid bounded adjudication after at most three launcher rounds.
- AC-2: The adjudication separately binds the final promotion SHA/tree/digest and every ordered reviewed-subject/launcher/findings digest, plus cycle ID, review-log digest, and deterministic cap result.
- AC-3: Every terminal finding is reconciled exactly once; missing, duplicate, unknown, or severity-mismatched entries fail.
- AC-4: Any terminal Critical fails; every High has a disposition, justification, and hash-verified evidence. Every terminal rubric failure is enumerated exactly once, mapped to dispositioned terminal findings, and hash-evidenced; unread dependencies and failed certifications still block.
- AC-5: A stale promotion target, altered reviewer bytes, hidden or attempted fourth receipt, fabricated cycle, reordered/omitted revision, or raw fail without adjudication fails closed.
- AC-6: Existing valid raw `pass` and `pass-with-findings` receipts remain valid.
- AC-7: The immutable HoursHub three-round plan cycle (successive manifest digests, terminal two High/two Medium, rubric failures 2/6/7/10, zero Critical) validates without another review or reviewer-artifact mutation.
