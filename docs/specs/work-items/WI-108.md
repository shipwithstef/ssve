## Goal

on-demand skill that answers the builder's load-bearing question: "why have I not shipped a revenue product despite owning this framework?" Produces an evidence-graded blocker report citing: resources under-deployed, projects half-finished, behaviors visible in `pipeline-decisions.jsonl` (e.g. skill-hopping, premature-completion, unused credits).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given P1.1 + P1.2 outputs + the session-level `pipeline-decisions.jsonl`, the diagnosis names ≥3 concrete blockers with evidence paths.
- No platitudes — every blocker anchored to a file path + line or a decision-log entry.
- If the diagnosis is ambiguous, it says "I cannot distinguish X from Y without more data" rather than fabricating confidence.

## File Impact

diagnostic only. Proposes at most the smallest sequence of 3 moves the ship-lens (P1.3) would recommend. No auto-execution.

**Size estimate:** medium, ~1-1.5 days.

## Cross-cutting decisions deferred

- **Continuous / daemon mode.** All 5 children are on-demand. A future WI can layer a scheduled invocation (cron, pre-prompt hook) once the on-demand variants prove useful.
- **Cash-reinvestment threshold.** P1.3 exposes this as a config knob; choosing the actual threshold is a builder decision, not a framework decision.
- **Skill vs extension of existing skill.** P1.3 names a new skill (`capability-concierge`) but P1.5 may fold into `assess-market-readiness` or `roadmap-evaluation` depending on how their evidence-grading contracts align. That decision belongs in `validate-feature`, not here.
