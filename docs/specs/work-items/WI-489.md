## Problem

The promoted WI-488 launcher pins Claude schema reviews to `--max-turns 1`. Two real WI-486 Fable 5/high plan reviews generated one provider inference but terminated at the internal structured-output tool handshake with `error_max_turns`, no findings, and the misleading classification `unknown_provider`.

Separately, Fable 5 can be provider-routed to Opus 4.8 by Anthropic safeguards. That provider-managed safety route is neither a normal Fable success nor the launcher's separately spawned availability fallback, and the current receipt/cache contract cannot distinguish it safely.

The owner also requires Opus 4.8/high to become the normal Codex-orchestrated reviewer at `2026-07-19T21:00:00Z` (`2026-07-20 00:00:00 EEST`, `Europe/Sofia`), with an easy explicit and receipted switch back to Fable 5/high.
