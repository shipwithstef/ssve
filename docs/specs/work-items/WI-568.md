# WI-568: Kimi preflight pause detection must respect negation context

## Problem

The Kimi UserPromptSubmit preflight hook treats every prompt beginning with
`don't` or `do not` as a pause request. Positive mandates such as `do not stop
until verification is complete` therefore disable autorouting even though they
instruct continued execution.

## Evidence

- `hooks/kimi/svc-kimi-preflight-guard.sh:50` includes bare `don't|do not` in
  the anchored pause-intent alternation.
- The matcher runs before active-lane discovery and emits `USER REQUESTED PAUSE`.
