# Problem Brief: WI-541 Full Framework Transition

## Problem statement

SSVE must consume every unique evidence-backed Example Marketplace-derived framework suggestion, implement the real gaps, reject or track the rest, and locally prove the result without weakening security, receipts, review, or installed-host truth.

## Upstream context

- Vision principles: progressive narrowing, worktree isolation, local-first operation, deterministic gates, zero-block same-owner recovery, fail-closed foreign mutation.
- Consumer constraints: S1 owner needs a lossless denominator; S2 router needs exact chain topology; S3 executor needs early safe routing and reachable recovery; S4 planner/reviewer needs mechanically true plans; S5 learning system needs measurable consumption.
- Key ACs: W541-02..03 exact chain; W541-04..08 authority/transport; W541-09..12 plan/review truth; W541-13..15 learning/envelope; W541-16..22 cleanup/correctness/consumption; W541-23..25 complete local proof.
- Journey complexity: seven system flows with foreign/path/replay/concurrency/malformed/absence negatives and an installed-host boundary.

## Success criteria

### Must

- Every W541-01..25 criterion has an executable consumer and a closeout proof.
- No foreign, ambiguous, cross-path, cross-WI, wrong-generation, or unsupported child mutation becomes authorized.
- No review, audit, security, full Tier 1, install, or final-SHA evidence gate is removed for speed.
- Historical proposals, receipts, authority state, and learning ledgers are preserved.
- The final result is locally committed and does not depend on GitHub.

### Should

- Remove repeated parsers and unused core context.
- Fail before doomed child launches and print one-hop recovery.
- Keep checks offline, deterministic, and low-latency.
- Make new machinery visibly consumed and behaviorally tested.

### Nice

- Reduce future proposal census and planning review time by making denominators and lifecycle outcomes first-class.

## Baseline approach

Add small pure enforcement primitives for mandatory chain topology, child transport, recovery capability, plan truth, learning lifecycle, and envelope decisions; wire existing compiler, hooks, skills, review launchers, landing, and validators; assemble sequentially and prove cumulatively.

## Assumptions to challenge

1. Shared pure modules are better than generating all contracts from `skills-manifest.json`.
2. The current controller and file-backed state remain the right foundation.
3. One plan-contract parser is better than independent validators.
4. Purpose-bound recovery can be added without a new authority store.
5. Normalize-on-read is safer than a ledger migration.
6. A full cumulative program is safer than separate residual WIs.

## Constraints

No new service/dependency/provider, no product code, no destructive history rewrite, no GitHub, no same-worktree child mutation, no scope drop, and no implementation prototype before the reviewed bulk manifest.
