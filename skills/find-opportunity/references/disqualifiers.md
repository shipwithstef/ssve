# Disqualifiers

Hard vetoes that reject an opportunity regardless of score:

| # | Disqualifier | Why It Kills |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Requires skills the builder failed at before** | Pattern from project history. Exception: if the failure was "couldn't code X" but now the pipeline codes it. The real question: can the builder MAINTAIN and DEPLOY it? |
| 2 | **Takes > 2 weeks to build at builder's pace** | Calculate real hours: builder's hours/week × 2 weeks = available hours. If MVP exceeds this, too big. |
| 3 | **No evidence of anyone paying for similar products** | "People should pay for this" is not evidence. Show a product, show revenue, show app store rankings, show paying customers. |
| 4 | **Requires business entity the builder doesn't have** | Stripe requires an entity. If no entity, must work with Dodo Payments, LemonSqueezy, or Gumroad. |
| 5 | **Requires upfront capital the builder doesn't have** | $0 budget means $0 spend. No exceptions. Not even "just $20/mo." |
| 6 | **Requires cold-start marketing the builder has no channel for** | If the only distribution plan is "post on Twitter" and the builder has 12 followers with zero engagement, that is not a plan. |
| 7 | **Builder has tried this exact thing before and it failed for the same reasons** | Check project history. Same concept repackaged with no new advantage = same outcome. |
| 8 | **Requires physical presence or serves local market only** | Online Product Mandate hard veto. If customers must be in a specific city, or the product requires the builder's physical time/location, it is out of scope. |
| 9 | **Evidence strength < 4 (weak signal or speculation)** | Don't present opportunities based on blog posts and hunches. Require at least one strong signal. |

When a disqualifier fires, log it and move on. Do not present disqualified
opportunities in the top 3. Do mention them in a "Considered and Rejected"
section so the user sees the work.
