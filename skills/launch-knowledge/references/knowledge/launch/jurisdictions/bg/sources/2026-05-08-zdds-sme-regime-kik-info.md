### Effective date and dual structure

Effective: **01.01.2026**

Two parallel SME regimes added to ZDDS:
- Domestic regime: **чл. 168д**
- EU-wide regime: **чл. 168е**

### Thresholds

> Domestic SME (чл. 168д) annual turnover threshold: **51,130 EUR**
> Applies to persons not registered under чл. 96, ал. 1 or чл. 100, ал. 1
> Automatic application if below threshold and not formally registered

> EU SME (чл. 168е): General EU-wide threshold: **100,000 EUR annual turnover across all member states**
> Individual member state thresholds: each country has its own "национален праг"
> If turnover in any single member state exceeds that state's threshold, the regime is lost only for that jurisdiction

### Critical interaction with чл. 97а ЗДДС (verbatim)

> "Регистрацията за режима за малки предприятия в ЕС (с '-EX' номер) се отнася само за извършените доставки."
>
> If a person receives services from abroad while using the "-EX" SME number, they MUST simultaneously register under чл. 97а, ал. 1 for incoming services. This creates dual registration requirements.
>
> Key provision: "новата чл. 97а, ал. 6 освобождава от задължителната регистрация по ал. 2... но не и от задължението по ал. 1"
>
> (The new provision exempts from mandatory registration under subsection 2... but not from obligations under subsection 1)

### Procedural mechanics

> Submit application per чл. 168ж (form pending from NAP)
> NAP has **35 working days** for review (чл. 168ж, ал. 8)
> Receive "-EX" number upon approval
> Regime effective from date of act delivery (чл. 168ж, ал. 14)
>
> Domestic regime: automatic, no special filing
> EU regime: **quarterly reports** (тримесечен отчет) per чл. 168л
> VAT declaration cadence changes from monthly to quarterly for EU-regime users

### Threshold monitoring

> Daily monitoring required for national thresholds in each member state
> Loss of regime is selective by country: exceeding one state's threshold causes loss only in that jurisdiction (чл. 168з, ал. 8), not EU-wide
> Exceeding 100,000 EUR aggregate threshold causes loss across entire EU

### Pre-2026 vs 2026 changes

> Prior regime (pre-2026): B2C sellers in EU above 10,000 EUR required OSS registration with VAT charged at destination rates
>
> 2026 SME advantage: B2C suppliers can operate VAT-free in other member states if below each state's threshold; no VAT charged to customers; quarterly instead of monthly EU reporting

### Incompatibility rules

> "Не може да се прилагат едновременно SME и OSS в една и съща държава"
> SME and OSS cannot apply simultaneously in the same member state
> Complete prohibition on combining SME with IOSS regime ("пълна забрана")
