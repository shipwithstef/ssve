Skill: assess-market-readiness

Q: What is the default assessment mode, and what is the realistic score ceiling for a first-time launch assessment?
A: Expected pattern: Default mode is `launch`. Realistic ceiling is 78-82; win condition is 70+.

Q: What are the 8 dimensions scored in assess-market-readiness, and which dimension has the heaviest weight in hackathon mode?
A: Expected pattern: Product completeness, user value clarity, competitive position, visual quality, technical reliability, monetization readiness, distribution readiness, market timing. Visual quality has the heaviest weight in hackathon mode at 25%.

Q: What is the required input artifact for assess-market-readiness, and what output file does it produce?
A: Expected pattern: Required input is `docs/specs/vision.md`. Output is `docs/specs/readiness-assessment.md`.

Q: When does launch-panel mode run, and where does its readiness score come from?
A: Expected pattern: Before strangers or money first touch the product (waitlist, first paid test, public launch, marketplace listing), or after a major landing change before it gets traffic. Not on an ordinary commit. The existing 0-100 score is computed from the panel evidence in references/launch-panel.md only after every required seat has a successful nonempty report. L0 build tasks create or reuse a WI and route through the matching lane. plan-changeset is next only when its baselined feature-spec prerequisites are already satisfied.
