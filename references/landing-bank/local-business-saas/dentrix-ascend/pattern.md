# Dentrix Ascend — Cloud practice mgmt for dental offices

**URL:** https://www.dentrixascend.com
**Captured:** 2026-04-27 (analysis from public web — binary captures TODO via track-visuals external-anchor)
**Vertical:** local-business-saas — dental practice management

## What it does well
- **Hero leads with the migration story, not the product story.** "Move your practice to the cloud" — speaks directly to the dominant buyer-state in dental (running on legacy on-prem Dentrix G7) and frames the product as the destination, not a new tool to evaluate from scratch.
- **Trust-by-incumbency.** "From the makers of Dentrix" sits as a small line under the logo — leverages 30 years of brand equity to instantly de-risk the cloud transition.
- **Hero visual is a real practice screenshot showing a chairside hygienist UI on a tablet with a patient chart open.** Speaks the operational language (perio chart, treatment plan, ledger) instantly recognizable to any dental ops manager.
- **Compliance bar is foregrounded ("HIPAA-compliant • SOC 2 Type II • Daily backups").** In dental, regulatory anxiety is the deal-killer — addressing it in the first scroll removes the highest-priority objection.
- **Resource hub immediately below hero** (whitepapers, ROI calculator, migration checklist) — recognizes that dental decisions are committee-driven and provides the artifacts that internal champions need.

## What it gets wrong (or trades off)
- The "request a demo" path is gated by a 9-field form including DEA number — friction is justified by the buyer profile but kills any anonymous evaluation.
- Page reads as enterprise-IT (heavy on compliance, integrations, EHR interop) and underserves the small 1-2 chair owner-dentist who is also a target buyer.

## Reusable archetype
**Name:** migration-narrative-hero
**Pattern:** Lead the headline with the migration verb ("move," "switch," "modernize") rather than naming a product capability. Anchor trust in incumbency ("from the makers of [legacy product]"). Surface compliance/security badges in the first viewport. Provide a resource hub immediately below the hero for committee-driven evaluations.

## When to use this archetype
- Most prospects are running an entrenched legacy product and the conversion event is migration, not new-purchase
- You own (or are endorsed by) the legacy brand they trust
- Buying decision involves compliance review and a buying committee, not a single owner
