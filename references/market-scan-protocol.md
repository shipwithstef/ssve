# Market Scan Protocol

## last30days Social Proof Discovery

### 4 Dimensions, 8 Queries

Run these searches fresh every time. Blog posts are NOT social proof. Reddit/HN/X threads with engagement metrics ARE social proof.

| Dimension | Query Pattern | What to Extract |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Revenue claims | "[product type] revenue [month] [year]" OR "how I made $[X] with [product type]" | MRR, ARR, unit sales, pricing |
| Launch validation | "launched [product type] on Product Hunt" OR "Show HN: [product type]" | Upvotes, comments, conversion signals |
| Community demand | "reddit [product type] alternative" OR "looking for [product type]" | Thread volume, upvotes, pain points |
| Organic traction | "[product type] organic growth" OR "[product type] without ads" | Traffic sources, viral mechanisms |

### WebSearch Supplemental

After social proof discovery, run targeted searches for:
- Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Funding data for comparable products
- Pricing benchmarks
- Competitor landscapes

### Reverse-Engineering Process

For each winner found:
1. What exact problem does it solve?
2. Who pays and how much?
3. What is the core mechanic/feature?
4. How did they get their first 100 users?
5. What stack/infrastructure do they use?

### Candidate Funnel Requirements

- Minimum 10 candidates investigated
- Minimum 8 categories scanned
- Each top-3 candidate needs 5+ evidence points
- Parallel research: run multiple searches simultaneously
