# Logo Exemplar Bank 2026

Use this bank for `design-logo` concept grounding. Copy principles, not visual treatments.

| ID | Reference | Transferable Principle | Anti-Copy Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | FedEx | Negative space can carry the brand idea without adding another shape. | Do not recreate an arrow or delivery metaphor. |
| A2 | Nike | A single asymmetric gesture can become ownable when it has confident motion. | Do not copy the swoosh curve. |
| A3 | Stripe | Repetition and rhythm can make a plain wordmark feel engineered. | Do not copy stripe cuts or diagonal wordmark styling. |
| A4 | Airbnb | A mark can combine community, place, and initial-letter cues into one simple loop. | Do not copy the Belo outline. |
| A5 | Figma | Modular primitives can communicate creative tools without literal UI chrome. | Do not copy the dot grid arrangement. |
| A6 | Notion | A restrained wordmark can rely on editorial personality instead of ornament. | Do not copy the cube icon. |
| A7 | Linear | A product mark can suggest speed and precision with minimal layered geometry. | Do not copy the diagonal line stack. |
| A8 | GitHub | A distinctive silhouette can outperform literal category explanation. | Do not use mascot logic unless the brief demands it. |
| A9 | Apple | Extreme simplification plus a tiny interruption can make a shape memorable. | Do not use fruit, bites, or glossy legacy cues. |
| A10 | Mastercard | Overlap can express exchange and relationship with almost no detail. | Do not copy the two-circle device. |
| A11 | Slack | A system of small parts can imply collaboration and motion across contexts. | Do not copy the hash or four-color language. |
| A12 | Spotify | Imperfect signal lines can make a simple container feel alive. | Do not copy the circle-with-waves mark. |
