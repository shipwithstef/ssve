## 2. Cross-domain implications

**Application to the Example Marketplace project context:**
* **IP Firewall (Freelance / Contracts):** Because the builder has a restrictive IP-assignment clause in their current trudov dogovor, they must formalize their work for Example Marketplace via a civil contract with the spouse's EOOD. This contract MUST contain an explicit "прехвърляне на имуществени авторски права" (IP transfer) clause. Without it, copyright defaults to the builder, making it vulnerable to the employer's claim. The explicit transfer establishes the EOOD's clean chain of title.
* **Liability Shield (Corporate / EOOD):** Since the SaaS processes local business data and payments (via Dodo Payments), personal liability is a massive risk. The spouse MUST operate as an EOOD, protecting the family's assets. The symbolic 2 BGN capital is sufficient but provides a complete liability shield.
* **EUR Transition (Corporate / EOOD):** If the spouse registers the EOOD in 2026, the founding act needs to either be formulated natively in EUR or actively amended to convert the 2 BGN capital into EUR before December 31, 2026, to comply with the 12-month transition grace period.
* **AI Tooling (AI / Tech):** Example Marketplace's General Terms (Общи условия) must explicitly disclose the use of AI in operations/scheduling and heavily cap liability for AI hallucinations. Ironically, the legal vacuum around AI copyright ("липсва човешки автор") slightly mitigates the employer's IP threat over AI-generated code, but leaves the EOOD with weak defensibility over pure AI outputs unless humans modify it.
* **Brand Protection (Trademarks):** The spouse's EOOD should apply for the EUIPO SME Fund *before* filing for the Example Marketplace trademark. This yields a 75% reimbursement (up to 700 EUR), cheaply securing the brand independent of the builder's employer.

## 3. Contradictions / open questions

* **Pricing Consistency:** LegalConsult.bg's T&C state "Всички цени са в лева и включват ДДС, ако адвокатът е регистриран", but the service catalog displays prices in both EUR (e.g., 125 евро) and BGN, and some explicitly say "без ДДС" (e.g., 450 лв без ДДС). This creates ambiguity about the final cost of forming the spouse's EOOD.
* **AI Authorship vs. IP Transfer:** The posts state AI-generated content cannot be copyrighted due to the lack of human authorship, yet also stress that freelance contracts must contain IP transfer clauses. It remains an open question how the spouse's EOOD can legally protect AI-heavy codebase sections if there is no statutory copyright to assign in the first place.
* The extracted sources are otherwise internally consistent regarding liability caps and statutory mechanics.
