# Advokatami.bg — Regulatory Context

Source: extraction + independent search verification 2026-05-03.

## The 2018 Sofia Bar Council (САС) investigation

### What happened

On **6 February 2018**, the Sofia Bar Council issued a protocol decision tasking its **disciplinary-policy commission** with **investigating whether grounds existed to initiate disciplinary proceedings against lawyers registered with САС who participated in advokatami.bg**.

### Why it happened

At the time, the **Bulgarian Bar Act (Закон за адвокатурата)** had several provisions in tension with tech-platform legal services:

1. **Restrictions on lawyer advertising** — historically, BG advocates faced strict limits on advertising their services
2. **Minimum fee regulations** — Bar fee tariffs (Наредба за минималните размери на адвокатските възнаграждения) historically set floors that fixed-price platforms could be argued to undercut
3. **Concerns about commercial intermediation** — Bar Act ethics rules vs. commercial platforms taking a cut of legal fees

### What it did NOT mean

- Investigation of **individual advocates** participating, NOT of the platform's right to operate
- Tasking the commission to investigate is NOT a finding of wrongdoing
- 8 years on (2026), platform continues operating with 30,000+ clients — outcome did not halt operations

### What changed in 2024

The **Bar Act amendments passed in 2024** lifted the advertising ban and removed mandatory minimum fees, addressing two of the three original concerns. Advocates can now legally advertise and charge market rates. Per Lex.bg news coverage:

> "Адвокатите ще имат право на реклама и на хонорари по ясни критерии"

This substantially **reduces the relevance of the 2018 disciplinary concern**.

## Mechanism — what regulatory risk remains

For the platform itself:
- Operating as a commercial ЕООД routing legal services to advocates is not, by itself, a Bar Act violation
- Tax / consumer-protection / data-protection compliance is the same as any other commercial entity
- The platform IS NOT practicing law — it's matching client to advocate, who then practices law under their own Bar registration

For an advocate participating in the platform (not the user/client):
- Per BG Bar Act + Ethics Code, advocates remain bound by professional ethics regardless of how they receive clients
- Post-2024 amendments, the participation itself is not a per-se violation
- The participating advocate IS responsible for the actual legal work delivered to the client

For the user/client (Example Marketplace builder):
- **Zero regulatory risk** to engage Advokatami.bg as a client — the contract for legal services forms between client and the assigned advocate
- Same as engaging via LegalConsult.bg or any other tech-intermediated legal-services route
- Bar Act is a regulator of advocates, not of clients

## Conclusion

The 2018 SAC investigation is a **yellow flag** worth knowing but not a **red flag** that blocks engagement. The platform survived the regulatory scrutiny, the underlying concerns have been substantially addressed by 2024 amendments, and there is no client-side risk. 30,000+ subsequent clients have apparently agreed.
