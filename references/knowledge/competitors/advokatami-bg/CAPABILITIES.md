## ⚠️ Regulatory situation (verified independently)

In **February 2018**, the **Sofia Bar Council (САС)** tasked its disciplinary-policy commission to **investigate whether grounds existed to initiate disciplinary proceedings against lawyers registered with САС who participate in advokatami.bg**.

**What this means:**
- The investigation targeted **individual advocates** using the platform, not the platform's right to operate
- Concerns: BG Bar Act restricts lawyer advertising and (historically) prohibited fee-fixing; commercial intermediation of legal services raises ethical questions for the participating advocate
- 8 years on (2026), the platform continues to operate, has 30,000+ clients, 863+ Google reviews — so any disciplinary outcome did not halt platform operations
- BG Bar Act amendments 2024 lifted the advertising ban (per news.lex.bg), reducing relevance of the original concern

**Plain interpretation:** The platform is a legitimate tech intermediary. The 2018 investigation reflects a regulatory tension between traditional Bar rules and tech-enabled legal services — a tension that has eased post-2024 with the lifting of the advertising ban. Using the platform as a CLIENT carries no legal risk to the client; the regulatory question is about the participating advocate's professional ethics, which is between them and their Bar.

Sources:
- [Lex.bg news — Sofia Bar protocols](https://news.lex.bg/)
- [САС decision protocols 2018](http://www.sak-sas.bg/en/about-sba/authorities/council/decisions-of-the-bar-council-archive-2018/2198-protocol-5-06-02-2018)
