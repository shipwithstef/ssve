## 2. Analysis (expert commentary)

**Rigor of Impact Assessment**
The 50% Net Impact Ratio is mathematically rigorous, calculated using the Upright Project's Net Impact Model (which processes data from over 200 million scientific articles, alongside databases from the World Bank, WHO, OECD, and IPCC). The formula strictly evaluates `(positive impacts - negative impacts) / positive impacts`. Achieving a 50% ratio is exceptional compared to traditional market indices—for instance, the S&P 500 typically achieves merely an 11% net impact ratio. This indicates that EIT Urban Mobility's capital deployment yields nearly five times the net positive societal and environmental outcomes relative to standard equity markets. By tracking direct, verifiable outcomes rather than self-reported ESG pledges, the metric maintains high structural integrity.

**EU Taxonomy Alignment Efficiency**
A critical indicator of investment discipline in EIT Urban Mobility’s portfolio is the unusually tight delta between EU Taxonomy *eligibility* (53.7%) and full *alignment* (38.5%). A gap of only 15.2 percentage points proves that the vast majority of portfolio activities qualifying under the Taxonomy's broad sectors successfully clear the stringent "Technical Screening Criteria" and "Do No Significant Harm" (DNSH) thresholds. In contrast, standard corporate benchmarks like the S&P 500 exhibit a massive drop-off (e.g., ~38.6% eligibility translating to merely ~6.8% alignment, a gap of >31 percentage points). This narrow spread demonstrates a highly efficient, anti-greenwashing capital allocation model where theoretical sustainability claims reliably translate into legally compliant, verifiable economic activities.
