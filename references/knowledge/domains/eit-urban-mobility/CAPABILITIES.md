# EIT Urban Mobility Financial Support Program

## Program Overview
EIT Urban Mobility is an institutionalized European partnership under the Horizon Europe Programme providing financial support to early-stage ventures. It aims to accelerate the transition to sustainable and inclusive urban mobility. By backing Pre-Seed, Seed, and Series A startups, the program amplifies high-impact solutions that reduce congestion, lower carbon emissions, and enhance urban accessibility.

## Program Capabilities
- **Financial Support**: Grants of up to €2.5 million per company. The total indicative funding allocated for the 2026-2028 period is €14 million.
- **Stage**: Startups currently engaged in an active fundraising round (Pre-Seed to Series A) with a prototype, pilot, or MVP. Pre-money valuation must not exceed €50 million.
- **Instrument**: Blended financing. Support acts as a de-risking grant linked to an active equity fundraising round where EIT Urban Mobility participates on customary market terms (targeting 5% - 10% equity).
- **RIS Benefits**: Approximately €4.5 million is exclusively earmarked for ventures in Regional Innovation Scheme (RIS) countries (Spain and Israel are evaluated under the non-RIS framework).
- **Non-Profit Reinvestment**: EIT Urban Mobility does not distribute dividends. All financial returns and proceeds generated through investments are irrevocably reinvested into the ecosystem to fulfil its public-interest mission.
