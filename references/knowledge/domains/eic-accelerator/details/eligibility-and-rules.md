# EIC General Conditions, Eligibility, Lump Sums, IP Provisions

## Mechanism

**Submission (all EIC calls):**
- Electronic only via the **Funding & Tender portal** (direct, or redirected from the EIC Community Platform); "Paper submissions are NOT possible."
- Part A online (administrative data, summarised budget, ethics/security questions); Part B downloaded → completed → re-uploaded as PDF (for the Accelerator, Part B = the evaluation-criteria questionnaire); annexes as PDF/excel.

**Country eligibility:**
- EU Member States + Horizon Europe associated countries (list on the F&T portal). Entities in countries *negotiating* association are in principle eligible if the agreement (incl. Pillar III) is in force at grant-agreement signature.
- Non-associated third-country entities: can join Pathfinder/Transition consortia where the EU/AC minimum is otherwise met; for the Accelerator can submit a SHORT proposal but must relocate/establish an SME in a MS/AC by full-proposal submission.
- **UK (from 1 Jan 2024):** eligible for grant funding — Pathfinder, Transition, and Accelerator grant-only. NOT eligible for blended finance or equity; grant-only UK awardees get no EIC follow-up equity; must relocate to apply for anything beyond grant-only; BAS access except investor-related services. UK is excluded from STEP.
- **No geographical quotas**: "there are no geographical criteria or quotas used by the EIC in selecting projects" — balance is pursued via NCP/EEN promotion, not evaluation.

**Concurrent / repeat applications:**
- Multiple EIC calls in parallel allowed; if the same proposal wins under several calls, only one is selected.
- Accelerator: only ONE Accelerator call at a time (Open XOR a Challenge); next submission only after results. Accelerator and STEP cannot be applied to simultaneously — "only the proposal submitted most recently will be evaluated."
- Ongoing H2020/EIC-pilot grants don't block new applications as long as activities aren't double-funded; Transition has its results-provenance eligibility; Accelerator grant-only beneficiaries can seek blended/equity-only (Fast Track possible for ongoing projects).

**Lump-sum regime (2026):**
- "In 2026, all EIC instruments will require a lump sum budget" — Accelerator Open + Challenges, Transition, Pathfinder Open + Challenges (Pathfinder calls need the lump-sum budget **annex**; AIC Stage 1 uses a pre-defined lump sum, no annex).
- EIC uses **Lump Sum Type 2**: detailed budget table (excel) annexed to Part B, costs resolved per beneficiary per work package; the table auto-generates Annex 2 of the grant agreement; totals must reconcile with Part A.
- Accelerator financial annexes: "lump sum detailed budget" (cost side) + "Financial plan and equity needed" (turnover, costs, cashflows, balance sheet, key ratios) used by evaluators to test whether resources are "realistic and timely"; 4-year balance-sheet forecast matters for plant investments/working capital; add columns if timeline is longer.

**Foreign interference / security:**
- Commission toolkit "Tackling R&I foreign interference" (doi 10.2777/513746) is recommended reading.
- Strategic-technology challenges (e.g. CRM 2026) carry ownership-control declarations; non-eligible third-country control → proposal rejected as ineligible.

**Generative AI in proposals:** allowed for structure and substance, but applicants "must be fully transparent... and declare the use of generative AI tools and the way the tools have been utilised"; full responsibility stays with the applicant.

**IP provisions (Pathfinder/Transition projects):**
- **EIC Inventor** = named inventor on a patent protecting an EIC-funded result, hired/subcontracted by a public-body EIC Awardee. Journal authorship ≠ inventorship.
- Ownership follows the awardee institution's practice; after a formal request the institution has **6 months** to decide whether to support protection/commercialisation — if not, the EIC Inventor may claim access rights (within the agreed Dissemination & Exploitation Plan).
- "Sufficient support" = funding patent costs in major jurisdictions, or expertise/infrastructure access; royalties shared on "fair and proportionate" terms that don't block the inventor's commercialisation.
- Transfers of result ownership / exclusive licensing: EISMEA may object; notification required.
- Awardees can suspend inventor access rights only on D&E-plan grounds; inventors can appeal to EISMEA. Penalties for non-exploitation only if the awardee opposes dissemination or withholds data.

**Misc:**
- EuroHPC supercomputer access available to EIC beneficiaries for AI/data-intensive work.
- European Innovation Gender and Diversity Index: bi-annual data framework measuring gender/diversity gaps across EU innovation/VC ecosystems.

## Analysis

- The dual-stage relocation rule (short proposal from anywhere negotiable; full proposal requires MS/AC establishment) is an explicit company-formation forcing function — relevant when advising non-EU founders.
- Lump-sum Type 2 moves audit risk from cost-reporting to work-package completion: budget realism is now an evaluation criterion rather than a post-grant accounting exercise.
- The IP regime protects individual academic inventors unusually strongly (6-month institutional decision clock + EISMEA appeal route) — relevant for spin-out negotiation leverage.
