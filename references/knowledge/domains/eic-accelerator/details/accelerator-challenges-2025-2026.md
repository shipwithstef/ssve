# EIC Accelerator Challenges — 2025 and 2026 Topics

## Mechanism

Challenges are Accelerator calls "open to proposals in predefined topics that have been identified in areas where breakthrough technologies or game-changing innovations developed by start-ups or SMEs can have a major impact on EU objectives." Same application pipeline as Open (see `application-process.md`); challenge proposals get topic-expert evaluators, challenge-specific juries, and portfolio-level support after award. Reserved budget per challenge; the applicant chooses Open vs Challenge (EISMEA does not transfer proposals).

### 2026 Challenges — total €220M, per-challenge €20-50M (EIC WP 2026)

1. **Advanced Materials for Renewable Energy and Energy Storage Systems** (WP2026 p.90) — scaling startups/SMEs developing advanced materials "specifically designed for energy storage or energy generation/harvesting systems" (solar cells, wind turbines, mid-long-term storage, energy harvesting); must minimize CRM use and environmental footprint with life-cycle analysis incl. cost and social impact.
2. **Alternative Concepts and Key Enabling Technologies for Fusion Power Plants** (WP2026 p.92) — budget transferred from Euratom; value-chain elements: magnetic/inertial/magneto-inertial fusion concepts, advanced materials, fuel production, laser tech, plasma components, magnets, advanced digital technologies, ICF targets; aims at commercialisation "expected in 2040."
3. **Biotech for Regenerating Agricultural Soils** (WP2026 p.95) — "about 60% of EU soils are considered unhealthy"; bioremediation technologies, soil/soil-microbiome management, renewable fertilizers and bio-stimulants.
4. **Boosting the European Critical Raw Materials value chain** (WP2026 p.97) — exploration, extraction/processing/metallurgy refining, recycling from end-of-life products; supports the CRMA. **Carries ownership-control restrictions** (declaration + control documents; non-eligible third-country control → ineligible).
5. **Deep Tech for Climate Adaptation** — extreme urban heat, climate-smart agriculture, water scarcity, flood/coastal protection; nature-based solutions + engineering reinforced by AI/KETs; feeds the EU Adaptation Mission portfolio.

### 2025 Challenges — total €250M, €50M per challenge (EIC WP 2025)

1. **Acceleration of advanced materials development and upscaling along the value chain** — four application areas: energy, mobility, electronics, construction.
2. **Biotechnology driven low emission food and feed production systems** — radically decreasing GHG emissions and environmental footprint of food production.
3. **Breakthrough Innovations for Future Mobility** — vehicle/vessel/aircraft/rail design+manufacture breakthroughs; heavy-duty operational-efficiency tech; sustainable fuels for aviation/maritime incl. retrofitting; digital tools from sensors to AI for autonomous transport, connectivity, capacity management, smart bi-directional charging.
4. **GenAI4EU: Creating European Champions in Generative AI** — startups either "further developing and validating new GenAI models", adapting existing models to sectors/data (smaller, faster, energy-efficient), or integrating GenAI in workflows with regulatory sandboxes, certification, post-market surveillance. Deep-tech bar: explainability, hallucination handling, transparency, trust, reliability, multimodality, model size.
5. **Innovative in-space servicing, operations, robotics and technologies for resilient EU space infrastructure** — in-orbit servicing & maintenance (RPOs, capture/docking, repair), in-space transportation (refuelling, LEO→GEO mobility, OTVs), space-based resilience (cybersecurity for satcom/nav/EO, SSA, debris).

### Historical note
The general challenges hub page still centers 2024 challenges (Human Centric Generative AI, Virtual Worlds, Smart Edge, Food from precision fermentation, etc.; €50M each under WP2024) — closed.

## Analysis

- Challenge topics rotate annually with the Work Programme; the 2026 set skews hard-tech (fusion, CRM, materials) vs 2025's digital tilt (GenAI, space). A pure-software builder fits only GenAI4EU-style topics, and only with genuine model-level deep tech.
- Challenge submission ≠ easier: same pipeline + sometimes extra eligibility (ownership control), but reserved budget and portfolio support tilt odds for in-scope proposals — EIC explicitly encourages in-scope applicants to pick the Challenge over Open.
