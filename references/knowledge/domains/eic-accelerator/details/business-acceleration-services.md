# EIC Business Acceleration Services (BAS) — Non-Financial Support Catalog

## Mechanism

**Who gets BAS:** EIC awardees (Pre-accelerator, Accelerator, Transition, Pathfinder, STEP Scale Up, incl. legacy SME Instrument/FET), EIC Accelerator **Step-1 successful applicants** (dedicated remote coaching), EIC Scaling Club members, and the "Extended EIC family": Horizon Europe Seal of Excellence holders, STEP Seal holders, Women TechEU awardees, beneficiaries of the Ukrainian tech SMEs support call. Access via the **EIC Community Platform** → "Open Calls" → apply. Investor-outreach results to date: "€350 million raised."

**Programme catalog:**

| Programme | What it gives | Cost / numbers |
|---|---|---|
| **EIC Coaching** | 1:1 coaching from 600+ vetted coaches; from Accelerator Step-2 prep onward; chemistry call → select | Free |
| **EIC Community Programme** | Peer-learning workshops, Winter/Summer Schools, talks, Info Days | Free |
| **EIC Tech to Market (T2M)** | Entrepreneurial Trainings (Foundation 18 May 2026, Immersion 24 Aug 2026, Validation 18 May 2026) + Venture Building Services (team formation, business intelligence, legal/accounting, IPR + financial advisory; cycles Mar-Jan) for Pathfinder/Transition/SoE/Women TechEU | Services covered; travel/accommodation on participant |
| **EIC Ecosystem Partnership / ACCESS+** | Service catalog of accelerators, incubators, research orgs + co-financing | **50% of service cost up to €60,000** |
| **EIC Corporate Partnership** | Scouting → Corporate Days matchmaking → mentorship → dealmaking with corporates (>€1bn turnover, >1000 employees, ≥10 EU MS presence; Health/Biotech, Energy/Sustainability, Digital/ICT) | Free |
| **EIC Global Business Expansion** | Soft-landing missions (e.g. South Korea 2026, deadline 15 Apr); external-expert selection on market-entry plan, impact, fit, resources, EU strategic autonomy | Free (scale-ups) |
| **EIC Innovation Procurement (SPIN4EIC + InnoMatch)** | Buyer community, tender assistance; InnoMatch funds **38 pilots up to €60,000 each** (12-month pilots; 3 call types pairing buyers and EIC beneficiaries; runs to Sep 2027; SPIN4EIC open until May 2026 innovators / Apr 2026 buyers) | Pilot funding up to €60K |
| **EIC International Trade Fairs 3.0** | EU pavilion presence at CES (Jan), MWC (Mar), GITEX Africa (Apr), BIO (Jun), GITEX Europe (Jun), MEDICA (Nov), GITEX Global (Dec); calls open ~6 months before each fair; pre-departure briefings, cultural/IP coaching, B2B matchmaking | Participation support covered |
| **EIC Scaling Club** | Curated community of **120 European deep-tech scale-ups** (Agri/Food, Batteries, Cardiovascular, Clean Fuels, Biotech, New Space); roadshows London 21 Apr 2026, Amsterdam 10-11 Jun 2026 | EIC-funded |
| **EIC Women Leadership** | Training + mentoring + networking; entrepreneurial cohort (C-suite women scaling ventures) + researcher cohort; 2026 closed (deadline 19 Apr 2026), 2027 EoI open | Free |
| **InnoNext** | **Fully-funded internships (3-6 months)** placing EIC Pathfinder postdocs, ERC researchers, MSCA fellows into EIC startups; AI matchmaking; "no financial burden for hosting companies" | Free to hosts |
| **RITA database** | Public database of EIC portfolio companies (filter by sector/country/service category) | Public |
| **Skills hub** | Entry page bundling Coaching + Community + T2M | Free |

## Analysis

- BAS is the EIC's stickiest perk: even a failed-at-interview company with a Seal of Excellence keeps coaching, community, procurement, and trade-fair access — which materially changes the expected value of applying.
- The €60K ACCESS+ co-financing and €60K InnoMatch pilots are quasi-financial instruments hiding inside "services" — combined they can be worth more than a Pre-Accelerator grant tranche.
- Step-1 GO unlocking free coaching means the rational path is: submit short proposal early, use the free coach for the full proposal.
