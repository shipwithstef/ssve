# EIC Identity, Prizes, Women TechEU

## Mechanism

**EIC identity:**
- The European Innovation Council operates under Horizon Europe with a **€10.1 billion** budget (2021-2027), "to identify, develop and scale up breakthrough technologies and game-changing innovations."
- Site and programmes managed by **EISMEA** (European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency) on behalf of the European Commission. No commercial imprint (no EIK/VAT) — EU institutional site; fraud reporting via OLAF; prize contacts contact-9371a6e7dd@example.invalid / contact-9c13692972@example.invalid.
- Work Programmes 2025 and 2026 published as PDFs from the about page.

**European Prize for Women Innovators (powered by EIC + EIT), 2026 edition:**
- Categories and amounts (verbatim): EIC Women Innovators — 1st **€100,000**, 2nd **€70,000**, 3rd **€50,000**; EIC Rising Innovators (under 35) — 1st **€50,000**, 2nd **€30,000**, 3rd **€20,000**; EIT Women Leadership Award — **€50,000** / €30,000 / €20,000.
- Eligibility: natural persons (women), founder or co-founder of a company legally established in an EU MS or HE Associated Country, company registered ≥2 years before the call year (before 1 Jan 2023); Rising Innovators must be under 35 at the start of the call year.
- 2026 cycle timeline: applications closed **25 Sep 2025** (17:00 CET); evaluation Oct-Dec 2025; jury hearings Jan-Feb 2026; finalists Feb-Mar 2026; award ceremony **June 2026**. Via Funding & Tenders Portal; Rules of Contest published. Status: Closed (annual cadence — expect a 2027 cycle).

**EIC Prizes hub:** European Innovation Procurement Awards, European Capital of Innovation Awards (iCapital), European Prize for Women Innovators, European Social Innovation Competition, legacy EIC Horizon Prizes (fuel-from-sun, batteries, etc.) — mostly recognition prizes for ecosystems/cities, not startup funding.

**Women TechEU (pilot, now under European Innovation Ecosystems):**
- **€75,000 individual grants** to early-stage deep-tech startups "founded, or co-founded, by women holding a top management position (CEO, CTO or equivalent — CFO/COO accepted)" at submission.
- Company ≥6 months registered in MS/AC; "early-stage" indicatively ≤6 years old and ≤€1M raised pre-MVP (more raised ≠ excluded, but evaluators prioritise those who most benefit).
- Application: written proposal (any EU language) + **mandatory 3-minute video pitch in English** (uploaded externally, link in Part B); one application per company (co-founders apply jointly via a lead applicant); financing "not linked to costs" (no lump-sum table); project duration indicatively 6-12 months; grant goes to the company, not the founder.
- Funds usable freely for growth: business-model upgrade, validation, certifications, IP/FTO strategy, market analysis, finding partners/investors. Winners also join the Women Leadership Programme (mentoring, coaching, networking, InvestEU/EEN activities).
- De Minimis does NOT apply (centrally managed EU funding ≠ state aid). Double EU funding of the same activities prohibited; parallel applications allowed with a double-funding declaration.
- Status: pilot closed (results early 2022); FAQ kept for reference; successor activity lives under European Innovation Ecosystems / EISMEA.

## Analysis

- The prizes layer is reputational, not a funding strategy — except Women Innovators (real cash for established women-founded companies) and Women TechEU-style instruments when reopened.
- Women TechEU's "financing not linked to costs" model is the lightest-bureaucracy EU instrument seen in this extraction — a template for what minimal EU grant compliance looks like.
