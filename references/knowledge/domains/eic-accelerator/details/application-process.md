# EIC Accelerator — Application Process, Timelines, Cut-offs

## Mechanism

**Step 1 — Short proposal (continuously open):**
- 12-page application form + 10-slide pitch deck (PDF) + 3-minute video pitch
- Submitted any time; "batched for evaluation every first Tuesday of the month at 5pm Brussels time"
- Result (GO/NO-GO) "within approximately 4-6 weeks"
- Funding amounts at this stage are indicative and non-binding
- A GO entitles the applicant to free EIC Business Acceleration Services coaching for full-proposal prep
- Recommended lead time: submit short proposal "at least 10 weeks ahead" of the intended full-proposal batch

**Step 2 — Full proposal (continuously open, batched every 2 months):**
- 2026 batching dates (17:00:00 CET): **7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, 4 Nov 2026**
- Submit ≥48h before the batch date to avoid technical risk; late submissions roll silently to the next batch (no error message)
- Via EU **Funding and Tenders Portal**; Part A online (admin/budget/ethics), Part B = 20-page questionnaire answering the evaluation criteria
- Mandatory uploads: pitch deck (no slide limit; 10-minute presentation if interviewed), video (≤3 min), lump-sum detailed budget (grant/blended), implementation plan (≤10 pages, work packages + deliverables + milestones), "Financial plan and equity needed" (incl. 4-year balance-sheet forecast; columns may be added for longer timelines), FTO analysis results (or ≤2-page note; simple statement where irrelevant e.g. software), letters of intent, ownership control declaration (CRM challenge scope, grant-only)
- Remote-stage result "within 8-9 weeks" of the batch date; the reply includes interview invitation status (not the panel report)
- GO at short proposal under WP2025/WP2026 → full proposal may be submitted any time in 2026/2027 (post-29-Oct-2024 short applications)

**Step 3 — Jury interview:**
- 45 minutes: 10-minute pitch (same deck as full proposal) + 35-minute Q&A
- Panel: max 6 jury members; EIC Programme Managers and EIC Fund reps may attend and ask questions but don't vote
- Brussels or remote; binary GO/NO-GO; results "within 2–3 weeks of the end of the interview week"
- Attendees: max 3 company representatives — employed staff, board members, investors only. "Consultants or other third parties must not take part." Costs not reimbursed.
- Companies may flag conflict-of-interest concerns about the assigned tech expert before the interview

**Step 4 — Award:** sign grant component; EIC Fund due diligence starts for the investment component (see `eic-fund-investments.md`).

**Resubmission rules:**
- Max **3 unsuccessful applications** (rejections) at any stage, any funding form, counted from 1 Jan 2024 to end of Horizon Europe (2027); pre-2024 attempts reset to zero
- Short-proposal GO does not count; interview failure after remote-stage success counts as ONE
- Withdrawal before receiving results does not count
- No proactive warnings; rejection letters state when the limit is hit
- Evaluators never see prior submissions or prior evaluation reports; juries see only the current full proposal + its tech-expert/panel comments
- Redress: procedural errors only (rejection letter names the procedure; cannot challenge scientific assessment) — Work Programme section "E. EVALUATION REVIEW PROCEDURE"

**Fast Track scheme (skip Step 1):**
- For projects from existing Horizon Europe / Horizon 2020 funding: ongoing EIC Pathfinder/Transition (incl. H2020 FET), EIT KIC schemes (EIT Health, Raw Materials, Climate-KIC, **Urban Mobility**, Digital, Manufacturing, Food), Eurostars-2 / Innovative SMEs partnership, and ongoing grant-only Accelerator projects (assessment ≥5 months before project end)
- The funding body performs a project review using criteria equivalent to the short-application stage → direct access to full proposal
- Same resubmission rules as short proposals; full-stage treatment identical and anonymous to evaluators

**Plug-In scheme (skip Step 1, national route):**
- Same as Fast Track but for **certified national/regional programmes**: "50 programmes from 21 Member States and 3 Associated Countries are now certified (including 16 programmes certified 'with conditions')"
- Certified countries include Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Israel, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Spain, Sweden + Montenegro, Norway, Türkiye, UK. (Bulgaria NOT on the certified list at extraction time.)
- Contact the national funding body managing the certified programme to enter

**Seal of Excellence:**
- Awarded when a full proposal scores **≥13/15** but isn't invited to interview, or receives NO-GO at interview
- Digitally sealed (fraud-proof) quality label signed by two Commissioners; unlocks alternative funding (national programmes, ERDF, private) and EIC BAS access
- Activation requires consent to share basic proposal data with NCPs / EEN / national authorities; EEN is contracted to help SoE holders find funding

## Analysis

- Wall-clock best case from short-proposal submission to interview result ≈ **5-6 months** (4-6 wks short + prep + 8-9 wks remote + interview cycle). Plan runway accordingly.
- The 3-rejection lifetime cap makes premature applications expensive — the EIC's own advice is to use NCP/coaching first and submit "when fully ready."
- The Fast Track list is strategically interesting: an EIT Urban Mobility-funded project (see `domains/eit-urban-mobility`) is a certified side-door into the Accelerator's full-proposal stage.
- GenAI may be used to prepare proposals but use must be declared; applicant bears full responsibility.
