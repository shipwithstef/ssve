# EIC Non-Accelerator Programs — Pathfinder, Transition, Pre-Accelerator, STEP Scale Up, AIC Pilot

## Mechanism

### EIC Pathfinder (TRL 1-4)
- "Funding research to develop the scientific basis to underpin breakthrough technologies... the earliest stages of scientific, technological or deep-tech R&D"
- **Grants up to €4M** + Booster grants (€50K); 2026: Open **€166M** (deadline **12 May 2026**), Challenges **€96M** (deadline **28 Oct 2026**), total €262M
- Eligibility (Open): consortia of ≥3 independent legal entities — ≥1 in a Member State, ≥2 others in different MS/ACs. Challenges additionally allow single applicants and small (2-partner) consortia
- 2026 Challenge topics (Challenge Guides published 10 Nov 2025): Advanced Materials for Miniaturised Energy Harvesting Systems; Biotechnology for Healthy Ageing; Deep Reasoning, Abstraction & Planning towards trustworthy Cognitive AI Systems
- 2025 Challenge topics: Biotech for Climate Resilient Crops and Plant-Based Biomanufacturing; Generative-AI based Agents to Revolutionize Medical Diagnosis and Treatment of Cancer; autonomous robot collectives for construction; Waste-to-value devices
- Portfolio strategic plans: EIC pro-actively manages funded-project portfolios (e.g. In-space solar energy harvesting, DigiTro, clean cooling, Precision Nutrition, Cardiogenomics)

### EIC Transition (lab → application, entry TRL 3-completed/4)
- "Maturation and validation of novel technologies from the lab to the relevant application environments"
- **Grants up to €2.5M** (+ €50K Boosters); 2026 budget **€100M**; deadline **16 Sep 2026**; interviews ~11-13 weeks post-deadline; results ~4 weeks from interviews
- Eligibility: mono-beneficiary (startup, SME, or research org) OR 2-entity small consortia OR 3-5-entity consortia
- **Restricted input funnel** — proposals must build on results from: EIC Pathfinder projects, ERC Proof of Concept, H2020/Horizon Europe RIAs, EDF research (civil applications only). An eligible-projects list is published ("EIC Transition - List of eligible projects 2026", 28 Apr 2026)
- 3 steps: written proposal (Part B max 22 pages) → remote evaluation → online jury interview

### EIC Pre-Accelerator (widening countries; TRL 4 → 6)
- "Boost the innovation potential of early-stage deep-tech startups in widening countries by enhancing their business, investor, and technology readiness" — joint EIC + WIDERA scheme
- **Single SMEs established in widening countries only**: Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia + all Associated Countries (Albania, Armenia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Faroe Islands, Georgia, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tunisia, Türkiye, Ukraine, and once associated Morocco and Egypt) + Outermost Regions
- Entry bar: "technology validated in the lab – at least TRL 4"
- Grants: **€300-500K (2025 call)** → **€500K-1M (2027 call)** at **70% of eligible cost**; budgets €20M (2025) / €40M (2027)
- 2027 call: opens **5 May 2027**, deadline **18 Nov 2027** (status: Forthcoming). Submission via Funding & Tenders Portal; Horizon Europe WP 2026-2027 WIDERA governs

### STEP Scale Up (strategic-tech scale-ups)
- Part of the Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform: "investments to startups, SMEs, and small mid-caps, between EUR 10 to 30 million... particularly in quantum technologies and semiconductors"; sectors: digital, deep tech, clean tech, biotech
- 2026 budget **€300M**; target funding round **€50-150M**; **proof of initial market interest from a qualified investor covering at least 20% of the total target funding round** required
- Eligibility: single SME or small mid-cap (<500 employees) in EU MS or eligible AC (**excluding the UK**); an investor may submit on the company's behalf
- Apply anytime; cut-offs 2026 (17:00 CET): **11 Feb, 6 May, 9 Sep, 25 Nov**; interview in 4-6 weeks from evaluation start; result ~2 weeks from interview
- Application pack: full business plan (≤50 pages), pitch deck (≤15 pages), investor pre-commitment, financial plan + agreement document, FTO analysis, key-personnel CVs; jury interview; full technological due diligence
- Investment flexibility margin: +€5M above requested amount (vs +€2M for Accelerator)

### Advanced Innovation Challenges — Pilot (2026)
- "Support high-risk, demand-driven deep tech innovation with transformative potential especially in areas where there is lack of commercial uptake"
- Two-stage lump-sum model: **Stage 1 = €300K for up to 9 months** (feasibility/benchmarking); **Stage 2 = up to €2.5M for up to 2.5 years** (development + real-world testing) — Stage 2 open only to Stage-1 winners
- Stage 1 eligibility: mono-beneficiary startup/SME/research org incl. individual PIs and inventors; larger companies excluded as single applicants. Stage 2: single entity or consortium of max 3
- Stage 1 deadline passed: 26 Feb 2026 17:00 Brussels (closed); Stage 2 indicative deadline: **18 Jun 2027**; total budget €6M

### EIC 2026 Work Programme (umbrella)
- Adopted **6 November 2025**; total ≈ €1.4B/yr across instruments
- Documents published: full WP PDF, "main elements", "companies", "researchers" briefs
- Headline 2026 changes: Accelerator forms 50→20 pages, evaluations every 2 months, deeper tech assessment pre-due-diligence; all instruments lump-sum

## Analysis

- The instruments form a TRL ladder: Pathfinder (1-4) → Transition (4-6) → Accelerator (5-9) → STEP (post-revenue scale). Each later stage has a Fast-Track-style funnel from the earlier one (Transition requires Pathfinder/ERC/RIA results; Accelerator Fast Track accepts ongoing Pathfinder/Transition projects).
- **Pre-Accelerator is the single most accessible EIC entry for a Bulgarian solo founder**: mono-SME, 70% funding, €0.5-1M — but it still demands lab-validated deep tech (TRL 4+) and the next window is mid-2027.
- Pathfinder is consortium-shaped (research orgs); not a solo-founder instrument except via Challenge single-applicant routes.
- STEP's 20% investor pre-commitment makes it effectively a co-investment instrument for companies already raising €50M+ rounds.
