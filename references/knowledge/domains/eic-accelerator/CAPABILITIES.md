# EIC (European Innovation Council) Funding Portfolio — lead: EIC Accelerator

## Program Overview
The European Innovation Council (EIC), under Horizon Europe with a 2021-2027 budget of **€10.1 billion**, identifies, develops and scales breakthrough technologies and game-changing innovations. Managed by EISMEA (European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency); equity invested via the **EIC Fund** (an AIF with an external AIFM; EIB is the investment adviser). Extraction source: eic.ec.europa.eu (64 pages, 2026-06-07). All EIC submissions run through the **EU Funding & Tenders Portal** — no paper, no fees.

## Key Capabilities & Rules
- **Accelerator 3-step process**: (1) short proposal — 12-page form + 10-slide deck + 3-min video, results in 4-6 weeks; (2) full proposal — 20-page questionnaire + lump-sum budget + financial plan + FTO analysis + LOIs, remote result in 8-9 weeks; (3) 45-min jury interview (10-min pitch + 35-min Q&A, max 6 jury members, in Brussels or remote), result in ~2-3 weeks. Consultants are BANNED from interviews; employees, board members, investors only.
- **Resubmission limit**: max **3 rejections** total (any stage, any form) from 1 Jan 2024 until end of Horizon Europe (2027); GO at short proposal doesn't count; withdrawals before results don't count.
- **TRL gate (Accelerator)**: TRL 5 achieved ("validation in relevant environment") is the entry floor.
- **2026 WP changes**: full-proposal forms cut from 50 → 20 pages; evaluations every 2 months (was 6); in-depth tech assessment anticipates investment due diligence; equity-only no longer requires prior grant; **all 2026 EIC instruments use lump-sum budgets** (Type 2).
- **Fast Track / Plug-In schemes**: skip the short-proposal stage via project review by certified funding bodies — Fast Track for HE/H2020 projects (EIT KICs incl. EIT Urban Mobility, Eurostars-2, ongoing Pathfinder/Transition/Accelerator-grant projects); Plug-In for **50 certified national/regional programmes** across 21 MS + 3 ACs.
- **Seal of Excellence**: awarded at full-proposal score ≥13/15 without interview invite, or NO-GO at interview; digitally sealed quality label to unlock alternative public/private funding; consent activates data-sharing with NCPs/EEN.
- **EIC Fund mechanics**: direct equity (co-investment matched ≥1:1, target portfolio leverage ≥1:3) or convertibles/SAFEs for lower-maturity companies; investment >€10M only via STEP; flexibility margin +€2M (Accelerator) / +€5M (STEP) above requested amount; patient capital, exit case-by-case (IPO, buyout, secondary); economic-security safeguards for AI/semiconductor/quantum/biotech (HQ-stays-in-Europe clauses possible).
- **Country rules**: EU MS + Horizon Europe associated countries; UK = grant-only (no blended/equity); third-country applicants may submit a short proposal but must establish/relocate an SME to MS/AC before full proposal; no geographic quotas in evaluation.
- **Business Acceleration Services (BAS)**: free coaching (600+ coaches, from Accelerator Step-2 prep onward), EIC ACCESS+ co-financing (50% of service cost up to €60K), corporate partnership matchmaking (>€1bn-turnover corporates), global business expansion missions, trade-fair participation (CES/MWC/GITEX/MEDICA/BIO), Scaling Club (120 scale-ups), Tech-to-Market venture building, Women Leadership Programme, InnoNext fully-funded internships, InnoMatch procurement pilots up to €60K.
- **Prizes**: European Prize for Women Innovators (1st €100K / 2nd €70K / 3rd €50K; Rising Innovators U35 €50/30/20K; EIT Women Leadership €50/30/20K); Women TechEU pilot was €75K individual grants for women-led deep-tech startups.

## Strategic Relevance for This Builder (BG-based solo software founder)
1. **Pre-Accelerator is the widening-country door**: Bulgaria is on the eligible list; single-SME, no consortium needed; €300K-1M at 70% co-funding — but next call opens 5 May 2027 and requires TRL ≥4 deep-tech.
2. **Accelerator demands deep tech, not app-level SaaS**: "game changing, could create new markets," TRL 5 validated, scale-up ambition; web apps without proprietary technology rarely pass the deep-tech gate.
3. **Plug-In via national programmes**: Bulgarian certified-programme beneficiaries can bypass the short-proposal stage — check the certified-programmes list before cold-applying.
4. **No fees, GenAI allowed in proposals** (must be declared); NCP/EEN support is free.
5. Cross-reference: `domains/eit-urban-mobility` (EIT ≠ EIC) — EIT Urban Mobility's KIC funding schemes are themselves a **Fast Track** route into the EIC Accelerator.
