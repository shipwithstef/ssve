- The easiest way to configure security is to tell the AI chat what you want.

- If it needs to change existing rules, it will ask for your approval before making any changes.
- You are responsible for your app’s security.
- The AI chat can help set things up, but always review your settings and run a security scan to make sure everything is configured the way you intended.

### SSO & Domains

**Setting up Single Sign-On (SSO) for your app - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Managing app settings Setting up **

- Notes: Single sign-on (SSO) is available for Base44 apps on the Elite plan or higher.
- To connect a provider such as Kakao through SSO, you need your own account with that provider and an app configured there.
- You are responsible for creating and managing the client ID, client secret, redirect URI, and any other credentials in your identity provider’s dashboard.
- To find your Base44 app ID and redirect URI: Go to your app editor in Base44 .
- Check your browser’s address bar and find the app ID between /apps/ and /editor/ in the URL.

**Connecting a domain to your app - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Managing app settings Connecting a domain to y**

- Base44 is an app builder that gives you 2 ways to set up a custom domain.
- You can buy one from Base44 for automatic connection, or connect a domain you already own.
- Before you begin: Custom domains are available on Builder, Pro, and Elite plans.
- Allow up to 72 hours for DNS changes if you use an external domain provider.

### Payments

**Accepting payments in Israel - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Selling on your app Accepting payments in Israel **

- With Tranzila, your customers can pay using credit and debit cards, Bit, Apple Pay, Google Pay, recurring payments, and installment payments.
- Important: You must be on a Builder plan or higher to accept payments with Tranzila.
- Tranzila is for accepting payments in Israel, and currency is in Israeli shekels (NIS) only.
- ​ Connecting a payment method Ask the AI chat to connect a payment method and follow the steps below to complete your Tranzila setup.
- ​ Step 1 | Connect payments Go to your app editor.

**Setting up Base44 Payments powered by Wix - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Selling on your app Setting up Base4**

- Connect Base44 Payments, publish your app, and start accepting payments without setting up a separate payment provider.
- To receive payouts, complete your Base44 Payments account setup and verification.
- How Base44 Payments works in Base44 Connect Base44 Payments using the AI chat in your app editor.
- Publish your app and go live.
- Start accepting payments from customers.

**Setting up Stripe - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Selling on your app Setting up Stripe Documentation Develope**

- You can ask the chat to install Stripe and create a checkout flow for your app.
- Everything starts in a safe Stripe test environment, so you can run test payments before you connect your live Stripe account and add your API keys.
- This flow was released in January 2026 and replaces the old Stripe flow.
- ​ Step 1 | Install Stripe Start by asking the Base44 chat to install Stripe for your app.
- Base44 sets everything up in a test environment so you can experiment without real charges.
