### Templates & Skills

**Creating workspace templates - Base44 Support Documentation Creating a workspace template Viewing your templates Updatin**

- You can create internal templates to keep your team’s apps consistent, or publish public templates so others can use or purchase your work.
- Important: To list any template, you must first publish the app.
- Only workspace administrators can create template listings for apps owned by that workspace.
- To sell public templates, you must have a Starter plan or higher.
- Publishing changes to your app automatically updates the workspace template.

**Managing workspace skills - Base44 Support Documentation Adding a workspace skill Turning a workspace skill on or off Ed**

- They activate when needed so the AI can follow your rules and best practices automatically across your workspace.
- Workspace skills apply to every app in the workspace for anyone who can work in that workspace.
- This helps you keep design patterns, copy rules, and data practices consistent, no matter which app you are building.
- You can use skills to give the AI specialized abilities for different kinds of work, such as: Design skill: Help the AI think like a designer by applying layout logic, spacing rules, hierarchy, and component best practices as it builds your UI.
- Copy skill: Help the AI focus on clear headlines, product descriptions, landing pages, and in-app text that match your tone and goals.

### Previous Model & MCP

**About the previous workspace model - Base44 Support Documentation Personal workspace Shared workspaces Workspace roles S**

- If your account has already been updated, this page may not match what you see.
- Read more about the updated workspace model in About workspaces .
- A workspace is your main hub for app development and team collaboration in Base44.
- Every Base44 account includes a personal workspace, and you can create up to 2 additional shared workspaces to collaborate with others.
- You can switch between workspaces at any time.

**Managing MCP connections - Base44 Support Documentation Navigation Managing your account Managing MCP connections Docume**

- You configure MCP connections once per account, and they are then available in the AI chat for every app in every workspace that your account can access.
- Base44’s AI treats MCP connections as tools it can call when your request requires external data or actions.
- It decides when to use them based on your prompt and the MCP’s description.
- You do not have to name an MCP explicitly, but it usually works best if you say what you want to use, such as “Use the GitHub MCP to list open issues.
- ” Each connection stores the server URL, authentication method, and any headers that need to be sent with every request.
