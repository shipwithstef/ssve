| Tier | $/mo | Adds vs lower tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $15.88 | Social media (FB+GBP) + SEO + AI post creation + unlimited scheduling + advanced analytics |

## Pro-tier Reviews capability — special note

The Reviews features (monitor / request / AI reply / display on site) are **competitive intelligence for Example Marketplace itself** more than a buy-decision. Example Marketplace serves local service businesses; those businesses live and die by Google reviews. Anything Example Marketplace ships that helps them manage reviews is high-leverage product surface.

→ **Action:** when planning Example Marketplace features, treat RelateSuite Pro's Reviews module as a **product spec to study**, not a tool to pay for.

## Trial origin: customer-perk vs public

The trial in this builder's case is a **customer-perk trial** offered because they already own domains at Namecheap — not a public sign-up trial. This matters:

- **Cancelling is zero-risk** for customer status. Domain registration and other Namecheap services are unaffected.
- **Cross-tier re-trial is MORE plausible** in customer-perk programs because the vendor's goal is upsell-sampling, not single-shot conversion. Public trials are usually one-per-email; perk trials often rotate or stack across tiers.
- **Possible future-perk path:** cancelling might trigger a Pro-tier perk offer later via normal lifecycle marketing. Speculative but consistent with vendor patterns. If a Pro perk-trial appears later, take it then — the Reviews module is product-inspiration only, so a delayed sample loses nothing.

Still: not documented in public terms. Support chat is the only authoritative answer.

## Decision unchanged

CANCEL the $9.88 trial. Even if Pro re-trial were available:
