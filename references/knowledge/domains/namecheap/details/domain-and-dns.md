# Domain & DNS

## Mechanism

Namecheap is one of the largest ICANN-accredited domain registrars (.com / .net / .org / hundreds of TLDs). Includes free **WhoisGuard** (domain privacy) for life on every domain registered through them.

## Pricing (2026-04-25)

| Service | Cost |
|---|---|
| `.com` first-year promotional | typically $5.98-$9.98 |
| `.com` renewal | **$15.88-$18.48/year** (plus $0.20 ICANN fee) |
| `.app` / `.io` / `.pro` / `.biz` | available — pricing varies, generally $10-$60/year |
| WhoisGuard domain privacy | **free for life** |

## DNS hosting

- Free DNS hosting for any domain registered at Namecheap
- Standard records: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, CAA
- DNSSEC supported
- Adequate TTL controls for production use

## Cheapest alternative

**Cloudflare Domains** or **Porkbun** — both register .com at-cost (no margin).

| Vendor | .com renewal | Free privacy |
|---|---|---|
| Namecheap | $15.88-$18.48 | yes |
| Cloudflare | $9.77 (at-cost) | yes |
| Porkbun | $10.37 | yes |

Saves **~$5-$8/year** to register/renew at Cloudflare or Porkbun. For a single domain over 3 years, that's ~$15-$24 savings.

## Relevance to Example Marketplace

**HIGH** — required to launch a professional service-business app. Without a custom domain, Example Marketplace can't have `contact-d159caf06f@example.invalid`, can't run on `app.example-marketplace.com`, and looks unprofessional to its target market (local service businesses).
