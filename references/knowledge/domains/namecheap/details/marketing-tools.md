# Marketing & Social Tools (RelateSocial)

## What it is

Namecheap's "Relate" suite — a centralized social-media scheduling dashboard with AI content generation. Comparable to Buffer / Hootsuite / Later but at a different price point.

## Pricing (2026-04-25)

| Plan | Channels supported | Notable features |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Facebook + Google Business Profile only | basic scheduling |
| **Pro** | Adds Instagram, X, LinkedIn | unlimited scheduling, AI content gen |

Specific monthly $ figures were not surfaced cleanly in the gemini-cli pass — verify via the Namecheap dashboard before any purchase.

## Cheapest alternatives

| Need | Free alternative |
|---|---|
| FB + IG scheduling, unlimited | **Meta Business Suite** — $0 forever, native to FB/IG |
| Multi-channel scheduling | **Buffer free tier** — 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts each |
| Multi-channel + analytics | Hootsuite free tier (limited) |

## Decision for Example Marketplace

**LOW relevance.** Pre-launch service-business marketing should be **direct sales** + **builder-network outreach** + **organic content**, not paid social-scheduling automation. Once Example Marketplace has actual customers and a content cadence, Meta Business Suite's free tier handles FB+IG completely, and Buffer's free tier covers a third channel.

Paying Namecheap for scheduling is paying for capability that exists free elsewhere.
