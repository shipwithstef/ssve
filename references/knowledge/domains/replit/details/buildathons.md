# Replit Buildathons

## Mechanism
Replit runs periodic buildathons to drive platform adoption and showcase Agent capabilities. Prizes are split between cash (immediate value) and credits (platform lock-in). Judging emphasizes creativity, Replit feature usage, and working demos.

## Past Events
- **Agent 4 Buildathon (March 2026):** 6,000+ participants, $57K prizes, 3 weeks
- **Mobile App Buildathon (Jan-Feb 2026):** $17K prizes, iPhone 17 Pro Max for top tier
- **MadeWithReplit (2022):** First hackathon, $10K prizes

## Replit 10 Buildathon (May 2, 2026)
- **Format:** 24-hour sprint
- **Prizes:** $100K+ total, Grand Prize $25K ($15K cash + $10K credits)
- **Special:** Free Agent for ALL users — no caps
- **Anniversary:** Replit's 10-year celebration

## Winning Patterns
1. Apps that leverage multiple Replit features (connectors + Design Canvas + multi-artifact)
2. Working deployed apps with live URLs
3. Creative/unique ideas over "yet another todo app"
4. Good visual design and UX
5. Community engagement (sharing progress, storytelling)
