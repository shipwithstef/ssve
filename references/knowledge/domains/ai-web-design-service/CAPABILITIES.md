# AI Web Design Service — Capabilities

Business model for building and selling animated websites to small businesses using AI-assisted tools (Claude Code + Motion + Nano Banana Pro). Extracted from X thread by @exploraX_ (2026-05-04).

---

## Overview

A micro-agency model that uses AI coding agents, React animation libraries, and AI image generation to produce scroll-driven animated landing pages for small businesses at a fraction of traditional agency cost and time. Positioned as an alternative to $10k+ agency builds.

**Target market:** Small businesses without websites or with outdated/generic sites (27% of US small businesses still lack a website per SCORE 2024).

**Value proposition:** Studio-quality animated websites in hours instead of weeks, at freelancer prices with agency-tier visual output.

## Key Validated Claims

### Market Stats
- **27–28% of US small businesses lack a website** (SCORE 2024, Zippia) — "1 in 4" framing is accurate

- **Agency custom sites: $10k–$35k** — Elementor, Dribbble, Allusive Digital confirm this range for agency-tier work

## Sales Strategy

### What Works
1. **Demo-first selling** — Loom/video demos outperform pitch decks; interactive demos increasingly replace discovery calls
2. **Tiered pricing** — maps cleanly to market expectations
3. **Content-as-funnel** — "build in public" on X/TikTok drives inbound; referral traffic converts at 10.99% (4× paid search)
4. **Case study flywheel** — each completed project becomes MOFU/BOFU content that compounds social proof
5. **Monthly retainers** — standard upsell path for web services

### What Doesn't / Needs Context
1. **"30 minutes per site"** — build time only. Real project timeline = days to weeks with client feedback
2. **Cold DM outreach** — ~1.8% baseline reply rate. Works only at high volume with personalization
3. **Flippa for templates** — WRONG. Flippa sells revenue-generating businesses, not design templates. Use Gumroad, Framer marketplace, or Creative Market instead

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor / Alternative | Differentiation |
|--------------------------|-----------------|
| v0.dev (Vercel) | Generates React components, but static/basic animations |
| Framer AI | Full site generation, but limited custom animation control |
| Wix/Squarespace AI | Template-based, generic output, no scroll-driven motion |
| Traditional agencies | $10k+ and 8–12 week timelines |
| **This model** | Custom scroll-driven animations + AI-generated hero assets + fast turnaround |

The **Motion animation layer** is the real moat — most AI builders can't replicate staggered scroll reveals, spring physics, and scroll-linked video playback easily.

## Risk Summary

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Claude Code pricing instability | HIGH | Budget for $100/mo Max tier; have fallback (Codex, Gemini CLI) |
| Client acquisition grind | HIGH | Invest in content funnel early; don't rely solely on cold DMs |
| Scope creep / revisions | MEDIUM | Fixed-scope contracts; charge for revisions beyond included rounds |
| AI-generated asset quality | LOW | Nano Banana Pro free tier sufficient; paid tier cheap ($0.14/image) |
| Market saturation | MEDIUM | Niche down (specific industries) to differentiate |
