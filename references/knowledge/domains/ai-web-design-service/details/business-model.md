## Scalability Analysis

### What's Scalable
- Template reuse — build once, customize per client
- Component library — reusable Motion animation patterns
- Content generation — AI-assisted copy and assets
- Deployment pipeline — Vercel automation

### What's Not Scalable
- Client communication — still human-time
- Revisions — client feedback is unpredictable
- Custom requests — each "small tweak" adds hours
- Sales — cold outreach doesn't scale linearly

### Ceiling
**Solo operator ceiling:** ~$15k–$25k/month
- 2–3 mid-tier sites at $5k = $10k–$15k
- 5–10 retainer clients at $300/mo = $1.5k–$3k
- Template sales (passive) = $500–$2k

**To scale beyond:** Hire project manager or transition to template/product business.

## Differentiation Strategy

The model's real moat is **not** the AI tools (anyone can use Claude Code). It's:

1. **Motion animation expertise** — scroll-driven reveals, spring physics, staggered entrances. This is harder to replicate than static AI-generated sites.
2. **Custom hero assets** — Nano Banana Pro-generated product visuals that match the brand. Templates can't compete with bespoke assets.
3. **Speed of delivery** — 1–2 days vs. 4–8 weeks for traditional agencies.
4. **Price positioning** — agency quality at freelancer prices.

**Sustainable differentiation requires:**
- Building a recognizable style/aesthetic
- Developing industry-specific templates
- Creating a content audience that generates inbound
- Establishing retainer relationships for recurring revenue
