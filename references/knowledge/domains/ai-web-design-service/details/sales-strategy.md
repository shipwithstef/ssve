# Sales Strategy & Go-to-Market

## Proven Tactics

### 1. Demo-First Selling

**Status:** ✅ VALIDATED

Showing beats telling for creative services. Loom/video demos attached to cold outreach consistently outperform pitch decks and text descriptions.

- Walnut (2026) documents the "Agentic Sales Motion" — interactive demos replacing discovery calls
- For cold outreach, soft CTAs ("worth a look?") outperform hard "book a demo" asks
- Loom allows prospects to see the site in their own time without scheduling friction

**Best practice:** Build a demo site for a prospect's competitor or similar business, record a 60-second Loom walkthrough, send with a soft CTA.

### 3. Content-as-Funnel (Build in Public)

**Status:** ✅ VALIDATED

Posting the build process on X/TikTok creates an audience that converts to clients over time.

**Conversion rates by channel (2025–2026 data):**
| Channel | Conversion Rate |
|---------|-----------------|
| Referral traffic | 10.99% |
| Facebook/Instagram Ads | 9.21% |
| Paid search | 2.9% |
| Organic social | 1–3% |

Referral (warm inbound) converts at **4× paid search**. Content + case studies build referral momentum.

**Content types that work:**
- Speed builds ("I built this site in 30 minutes")
- Before/after transformations
- Tool tutorials
- Pricing transparency posts
- Client result case studies

---

### 4. Case Study Flywheel

**Status:** ✅ VALIDATED

Every completed project should become a case study. This compounds:
1. First project → case study → credibility for second project
2. Second project → two case studies → more inbound
3. Inbound replaces cold outreach over time

**Case study format:**
- Problem (business had no website / outdated site)
- Solution (built animated landing page with AI-assisted tools)
- Result (specific metric if available — leads, impressions, compliments)
- Visual (screenshot/loom of final site)

---

## Tactics That Need Context

### Cold DM Outreach

**Status:** ⚠️ LOW CONVERSION

| Platform | Effectiveness | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| LinkedIn | Medium | Standard B2B channel; crowded |
| X/Twitter | Low-Medium | Works for creative services; requires existing audience |
| Instagram DMs | Low | Unconventional for B2B; lower buyer intent |

**Cold email/DM benchmarks:**
- Baseline reply rate: ~1.8%
- Top performer reply rate: ~3.9%
- Positive reply rate target: 0.8–1.5%

**Reality check:** To land one client, you may need 50–100 personalized touchpoints. This is a grind, not a shortcut.

**Better approach:** Use cold outreach for feedback/demo validation, but invest in content funnel for scalable acquisition.

## Recommended GTM Sequence

1. **Month 1:** Build 3–5 demo sites for different niches. Post builds on X/TikTok. No client work yet — pure content.
2. **Month 2:** Start cold outreach (LinkedIn primary, X secondary) with Loom demos. Offer first 2 clients a discount for case study rights.
3. **Month 3+:** Double down on content. Every client becomes a case study. Raise prices as social proof compounds.
4. **Month 6+:** Launch template products on Gumroad for passive income. Retainer clients provide recurring revenue baseline.
