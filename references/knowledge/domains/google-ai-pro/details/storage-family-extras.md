# Storage / Family / Extras

## Storage

**5 TB** across Drive + Gmail + Photos. Doubled from 2TB → 5TB on **2026-04-01** at no price increase.

By comparison: Plus = 200 GB, Ultra = 30 TB.

For a builder this matters mainly for:
- **Drive** — long-term repo of generated assets (Veo videos, Flow renders, Nano Banana images, NotebookLM exports)
- **Gmail** — never delete email; storage was a binding constraint for power users at 100 GB
- **Photos** — incidental

5 TB is essentially unbounded for non-video workflows.

## Family sharing

Up to 5 additional Google accounts can be added to the plan. Each shared member gets:
- Pro-tier Gemini access
- Pro-tier Workspace integrations
- Notebook + Deep Research tier limits
- 5 TB storage shared across the group

**Open question (per Google forum):** does each member also get the $10/mo Cloud credit individually, or is the GDP benefit single-claim? Verify before relying on it for distributed work.

## Google Home Premium Standard

**Bundled at no extra cost** with Pro (Pro = Standard plan; Ultra = Advanced plan).

Includes:
- 30-day event history on Nest cameras
- Gemini-powered features inside Google Home (intent recognition, scene generation)
- Standalone value: $10/mo if subscribed separately

Largely irrelevant to a software builder unless you also operate a smart-home setup. Captured here only because it's a genuine $10/mo offset against the €21.98/mo plan price.

## YouTube Premium

**NOT included in Pro** (Ultra-only). If YouTube ad-free matters, the math doesn't favor Pro alone.

## Total non-AI value floor

- 5 TB storage @ Google One pricing: would cost $24.99/mo standalone (2 TB plan)
- Google Home Premium Standard: $10/mo standalone
- **Combined non-AI value: $34.99/mo** — already exceeds Pro's $19.99 sticker price

Meaning: even if every AI feature were ignored, Pro is **net positive vs paying for storage + smart-home separately**. The AI is effectively free on top of those.
