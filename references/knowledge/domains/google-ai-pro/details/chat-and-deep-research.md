# Chat & Deep Research (Gemini app)

## Mechanism

Pro-tier access to Google's flagship consumer chat product (gemini.google.com / Gemini mobile apps). Quotas are per-feature, not pooled.

## Numerical limits (Pro tier, 2026-04-25)

| Feature | Pro | Free | Ultra (for context) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.1 Pro prompts | **100 / day** | limited | 500 / day |
| Gemini 3 Thinking prompts | **300 / day** | limited | 1,500 / day |
| Pool semantics | **Independent** (Apr 2026 change — not shared) | shared | independent |
| Context window | **1,000,000 tokens** | 32,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Deep Research reports | **20 / day** (~600/mo) | 5 / month | unlimited |
| Deep Search | included | not available | included |

April 2026 doubled the Thinking allocation (was 100 → 300 for Pro, 500 → 1500 for Ultra) and split the previously-pooled prompts into independent pools. Free tier was simultaneously cut by 50–92% on 2025-12-07 across multiple models.

## Deep Research mechanics

- Autonomous browse: web + (optionally) Gmail/Drive/Chat
- Generation time: 5–10 min typical, longer for complex topics
- Output: structured multi-section report with citations
- Pro daily limit (20) effectively bypasses any practical research bottleneck for a solo builder
- New tier: **Deep Research Max** introduced 2026-04 (Ultra-only) — autonomous research agent on Gemini 3.1 Pro
