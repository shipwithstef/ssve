# Generation — Veo, Flow, Image, Music

## Mechanism

Google's image/video/music generation models, accessible across multiple surfaces (Gemini app, Search, **Flow** filmmaking tool). Pro tier gives meaningful-but-bounded access; Ultra has the highest caps.

## Veo (video generation) — Pro tier limits

| Surface | Veo 3 / 3.1 (full) | Veo 3 Fast | Veo 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini app** | bounded | **~3 / day** (~90/mo) | ~5/day (~30/mo) |
| **Flow** (uses credits) | ~2 full Veo 3 / mo (50 credits each) | ~50 Veo 3.1 Fast (2 credits ea) or ~100 Veo 2 / mo | included in same 100-credit pool |

**Pro plan = 100 Flow credits/month total.** This is the binding constraint for serious filmmaking work. Ultra gets ~2,500 credits/mo and half-price Veo 3.1 Fast.

## Flow

AI filmmaking tool at [labs.google/flow](https://labs.google/flow). Custom-designed for Veo + Imagen + Gemini integration. Workflow: scene planning → shot composition → Veo render → assemble cinematic clips.

Pricing model: **credit-based, separate from chat quotas.** 100 credits/mo on Pro is enough for ~2 polished video pieces or ~50 short Fast clips per month.

## Image generation

- **Nano Banana Pro** — Google's image model, higher access for Pro tier
- Available inside Gemini app, Search AI Mode, and Flow

## Music generation

Higher access in Gemini for Pro (specific cap not published). Useful for content side-earning where licensed-music alternatives are pricey.

## Side-earning angles

| Angle | Resource cost | Stockpile-ability |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-form video for X / TikTok** | 50 Fast clips/mo at Pro = 50 posts | High — output is durable |
| **Polished YouTube cinematic** | 2 full Veo 3/mo on Pro | Low — single piece per month |
| **Image assets for blog/landing pages** | Nano Banana Pro | High — reusable |
| **Music beds for audio content** | Higher access | Medium |

Combined with **MiMo's free TTS suite** (V2.5-TTS, VoiceClone, VoiceDesign) the builder has a free-or-nearly-free **audio + video + image** pipeline as long as both windows stay open.
