# Lucas Patiri UGC Viral Growth & B2B Translation Heuristics

This detail file maps the core mechanics of Lucas Patiri's consumer app UGC growth playbook and provides the translation rules for applying these concepts to B2B outbound outreach (such as Example Marketplace B2B salon/restaurant outreach).

---

## 1. Core Mechanics of the Playbook
Lucas Patiri's playbook argues that **operational systems** dictate campaign success, not individual talent. The 10 rules target the most frequent operational failure modes:

*   **Rule 1: Standardized Briefing:** A high-performing brief must specify a one-sentence objective, 2-4 key messaging points, a detailed target audience description, explicit format/length requirements, and 3-5 visual reference videos.
*   **Rule 2: Averaged View Metrics:** Ignore vanity follower count metrics. Prioritize creators based on average view counts, hook quality, and communication responsiveness.
*   **Rule 3: Raw, Platform-Native Vibe:** Polished, commercial-looking content is skipped; raw, authentic videos drive organic user acquisition.
*   **Rule 4: Pre-Moderation Gating:** Review and approve every content draft before it is published.
*   **Rule 5: High-Frequency Feedback:** Respond and provide feedback to creators in less than 24 hours using a consistent correction vocabulary.
*   **Rule 6: Format Testing Cycles:** Regularly test 3-5 new creative formats weekly to prevent CPI fatigue.
*   **Rule 7: Aligned Performance Incentives:** Use performance-based clauses or Minimum Viable Contracts (MVC).
*   **Rule 8: Centralized Communication:** Keep all creator actions, feedback, and briefs in a single master sheet or channel.
*   **Rule 9: Mid-Funnel Auditing:** Measure Day-3 and Day-7 retention metrics by creator and format to identify actual quality.
*   **Rule 10: Performance Pruning:** Audits must offload underperforming (C-tier) creators monthly to free up capital.

## 2. B2B Outbound Translation Logic
To translate these consumer-facing UGC metrics to B2B cold outreach (DMs, emails), apply the following mappings:

### The "Native UGC" Copy Standard
*   **Mechanism:** Replace corporate, polished "sales pitching" copy with highly conversational, raw, and native outreach scripts.
*   **Rule:** DMs and cold emails must lead with a highly specific, observable hook (e.g., referencing a menu item, renovation, or a specific post on the prospect's profile) rather than a generic value proposition.
*   **CTA Rule:** Limit to one clear, low-friction, conversational question (e.g., "Punch cards or just vibes?") rather than asking for a calendar booking.

### Micro-Testing (MVC)
*   **Mechanism:** Rather than scaling a new outbound campaign immediately across hundreds of domains, deploy a "Minimum Viable Content/Contract" pilot batch (e.g., 5-10 DMs/emails).
*   **Rule:** Validate open rates and response rates before scaling. If reply rate is under 2%, pause, rewrite, and re-test in another micro-batch.

### Aligned Incentives & Retention Gates
*   **Mechanism:** Pay out referring agents or sample merchants based on down-funnel retention events rather than simple signups.
*   **Rule:** For Example Marketplace, payouts occur only after the referred salon has successfully scheduled their first 50 appointments or checked in 20 loyalty customers.
