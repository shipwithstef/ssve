## Core Frameworks

- **Bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM** — build market size from ICP count × ARPU × penetration; dual-method (top-down macro + bottom-up) is the 2026 VC-trust standard
- **JTBD Four Forces** — Push (frustration), Pull (new promise), Anxiety (risk), Habit (inertia); 10-15 switch interviews per segment reveals actionable patterns
- **Mom Test Protocol** — anchor every question in a past event ("Tell me about a time when…"); never ask hypotheticals; extract facts, not validations
- **Win/Loss Analysis** — continuous, cross-functional; structured review of won + lost + no-decision deals within 90 days of close; min 10 deals/cohort for pattern reliability
- **Competitive Teardown Grid** — four axes: Target (ICP), Promise (value prop), Proof (case studies/logos/metrics), Pricing (published or inferred + tier structure)
- **Opportunity/Threat Radar** — PESTLE × SWOT integration; macro signals feed O/T quadrant; updated quarterly minimum, monthly in high-velocity markets

## Decision Triggers

**Market sizing — flag as weak if:**
- SOM >5% of SAM in Y1 without evidence of existing traction
- Only top-down sizing presented (no bottom-up ICP count × ARPU)
- TAM sourced from a single analyst report without cross-check

**Competitive teardown — flag as incomplete if:**
- Missing any of: Target / Promise / Proof / Pricing axes
- Pricing page not checked within 30 days (70% of B2B SaaS change in 6mo)
- Win/loss reason coded from CRM only (70% of CRM data inaccurate within a year)

**Win/loss — flag as unreliable if:**
- <10 deals in cohort
- Interviews >90 days post-close
- No cross-functional distribution (only 39% of companies run cross-functional programs; those that do report 68% higher win-rate lift — Clozd)

**Customer discovery — flag as insufficient if:**
- <10 switch interviews per target segment
- Questions are hypothetical rather than past-event anchored
- Four forces (Push/Pull/Anxiety/Habit) not mapped for the switching moment

**NRR / churn — flag for investigation if:**
- SMB NRR <90% or >monthly churn 5%
- Mid-market NRR <100%
- Enterprise NRR <105%
