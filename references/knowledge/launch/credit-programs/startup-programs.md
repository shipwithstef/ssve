## How to Maximize

1. **Apply early** — most programs require <2 years old or <$5M raised
2. **Stack credits** — use multiple programs simultaneously
3. **Track expiry** — set calendar reminders for credit expiration
4. **Use before expire** — credits don't roll over
5. **Document everything** — save confirmation emails, credit amounts

---

## Application Tips

- **Have a landing page** — most programs require a public website
- **Show traction** — even early metrics help
- **Be specific** — explain what you'll use the credits for
- **Follow up** — some programs take 1-2 weeks to respond
- **Network** — attend startup events, join communities
