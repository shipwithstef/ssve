# EUIPO SME Fund — "Ideas Powered for Business"

Last verified: 2026-05-03. Sources: [EUIPO official portal](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/sme-corner/sme-fund/2026), Elzaburu, Eesti Consulting, LegalConsult.bg.

## What it is

EU-level reimbursement scheme for IP registration costs, run by the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) under the European Commission's "Ideas Powered for Business" initiative. Reimburses 75-90% of fees for trademarks, designs, patents, plant varieties, and IP scan diagnostics — up to per-voucher caps.

## Why it matters for software founders

- **Trademark protection for product/brand name** at 75% reimbursement (Voucher 2)
- **IP scan diagnostic** at 90% reimbursement gives strategic IP roadmap (Voucher 1)
- **Patent fees** at 75% reimbursement when applicable (Voucher 3)
- Eligibility is broad — self-employed individuals (свободна професия) AND companies qualify
- Application is direct to EUIPO; no Bulgarian intermediary needed (though IP agencies offer paid prep services)

## Voucher catalog

| Voucher | Covers | % | Max | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — IP Scan** | Strategic IP diagnosis + enforcement advice | 90% | €1,350-1,620 (varies per country) | Pre-filing strategy review |
| **2 — Trademarks & Designs** | National/regional/EU registration fees + 50% on Madrid/Hague international | 75% (50% intl) | **€700** | Most relevant for SaaS founders |
| **3 — Patents** | National + EPO filing + 50% on legal drafting | 75% / 50% | €3,500 combined | Less relevant for generic SaaS |
| **4 — Plant Varieties** | CPVO registration | 75% | €1,500 | Not relevant for software |

## Eligibility (verified)

- SME established in EU (any member state including BG)
- < 250 employees
- Annual turnover ≤ €50M OR balance sheet ≤ €43M
- **Self-employed individuals (свободна професия) qualify**
- Foundations with <25% public capital qualify
- No requirement that the trademark already be filed
- Both newly-established and existing businesses qualify

## Eligible costs (Voucher 2, primary use case)

✅ Application fees (BPO national, EUIPO EU, WIPO Madrid)
✅ Examination fees
✅ Registration fees
✅ Publication costs
✅ Search reports
✅ Professional representative fees (lawyer / IP consultant)

❌ Renewal fees for existing rights
❌ Costs incurred BEFORE grant approval (apply BEFORE filing the trademark)

## Application flow

1. Create EUIPO User Area account
2. Complete SME Fund application form
3. Submit eligibility documents (SME proof, ID, bank details)
4. Receive grant decision (~2-4 weeks)
5. **AFTER grant approval** — file the trademark/design at BPO / EUIPO / WIPO
6. Submit reimbursement claim with paid invoices
7. Receive 75% back to designated bank account

Direct portal: https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/sme-corner/sme-fund

## When to use

- Trademarking product/brand name in BG, EU, or international Madrid system
- Filing for industrial design (UI patterns, distinctive product appearance)
- Strategic IP diagnostic before scaling

## When NOT relevant

- Renewing existing trademarks (excluded)
- Already filed before grant approval (excluded — must apply BEFORE)
- Pure software where no trademark/design/patent fit exists

## Stackability

Stackable with all other launch credit programs — this is a national/EU IP grant, not a cloud/SaaS credit. Independent of Microsoft Founders Hub, NVIDIA Inception, AWS Activate, etc. No conflict.
