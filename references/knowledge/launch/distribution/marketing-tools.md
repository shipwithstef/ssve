# Marketing Tools — Free-First Strategy

**Last updated:** 2026-04-28
**Staleness:** 90 days (pricing changes frequently)

---

## Philosophy

**Start free, upgrade when you have revenue.** Most marketing tools offer generous free tiers that are sufficient for launch and early growth. Only pay when you've proven the tool's value.

---

## Analytics & Product

### PostHog (Recommended — All-in-One)
**What it does:** Product analytics, session replay, feature flags, A/B testing, surveys
**Free tier:** 1M events/month, 5K recordings, 1M flag requests
**Paid:** $0.00005/event after free tier
**Startup program:** $50,000 in credits for 12 months
**Best for:** Startups wanting single tool instead of Mixpanel + GA4 + Hotjar

### GA4 (Google Analytics 4)
**What it does:** Web analytics, conversion tracking
**Free tier:** Unlimited events, all features
**Paid:** Free forever
**Best for:** Google ecosystem integration (Search Console, Ads)

### Mixpanel
**What it does:** Product analytics, user segmentation, funnels
**Free tier:** 20M events/month
**Paid:** $0.00028/event after free tier
**Best for:** High-volume analytics, advanced segmentation

### Amplitude
**What it does:** Product analytics, experimentation, CDP
**Free tier:** 10M events/month
**Paid:** Custom pricing
**Best for:** Enterprise-grade analytics

## Key Principles

1. **Start free** — most tools have generous free tiers
2. **Apply for startup programs** — PostHog, AWS, HubSpot all offer credits
3. **Track usage** — know when you're approaching limits
4. **Upgrade when proven** — only pay when tool has demonstrated value
5. **Avoid vendor lock-in** — keep data exportable
6. **Stack credits** — use multiple startup programs simultaneously
