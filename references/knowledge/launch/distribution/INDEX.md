## How distribution composes with existing skills

`launch-knowledge` identifies the distribution **channels** appropriate for the product type and founder profile. The execution of the outreach is delegated:

- `cold-email` — multi-step sequences for cold outreach
- `prospect` — finding prospect lists (LinkedIn Sales Nav, Apollo, etc.)
- `ai-cold-outreach` — AI-personalized variants
- `launch` — Product Hunt / HN / community launches with timing + asset campaigns
- `lead-magnets` — content + free-tool offers as lead-gen
- `free-tools` — engineering-as-marketing pattern

`launch-knowledge` does NOT execute outreach itself. See `launch-knowledge/references/skill-composition.md` for the handoff protocol.
