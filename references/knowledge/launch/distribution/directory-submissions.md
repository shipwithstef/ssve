# Directory Submissions — Strategy & Priority List

**Last updated:** 2026-04-28
**Staleness:** 90 days (directory landscape changes)

---

## Why Directories Matter

1. **Backlinks** — High DR sites pass link equity to your pages
2. **Discovery** — People browsing directories are in-market buyers
3. **AI citations** — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity pull from high-DR directories
4. **Social proof** — Listings on G2, Capterra build trust

**Key stat:** AI-referred traffic converts 6-27x higher than traditional search traffic.

---

## Three Hard Rules

### Rule 1: Foundation Before Submission
Never submit until your landing page has:
- Single `<h1>` and sequential heading hierarchy
- Real pricing page
- Privacy policy + terms
- Logo assets (PNG + SVG + square 1024x1024 + favicon)
- 5-8 real screenshots (1920x1080)
- 60-90 second demo video
- FAQ schema markup
- Structured data (Organization, Product, SoftwareApplication)

### Rule 2: Destination Pages Before Directories
Create these before submitting anywhere:
- 3-5 competitor alternative pages (`/alternatives/[competitor]`)
- 3-5 use-case pages (`/for/[audience]` or `/use-cases/[use-case]`)
- Template gallery (if applicable)
- 1 "best of" blog post about your category

### Rule 3: Positioning Varies by Directory
Never copy-paste the same description everywhere:

| Surface | Lead with | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Startup directories | Outcome | Audience is founders |
| SaaS directories | Alternative framing | People search "[competitor] alternative" |
| AI directories | AI-first architecture | Audience wants AI tools |
| Agent/MCP directories | Agent/MCP angle | Niche but high-intent |
| No-code directories | Ease + power | Audience values speed |
| Dev directories | Technical depth | Devs reward substance |
| B2B review sites | ROI + use case | Buyers want outcomes |

## What You Need for Each Directory

### Startup Directories (Product Hunt, BetaList)
- Product name + tagline
- Description (2-3 paragraphs)
- Logo (1024x1024)
- Gallery images (5+)
- Demo video (60-90 seconds)
- Maker profile

### B2B Review Sites (G2, Capterra)
- Product category
- Feature list
- Pricing
- Screenshots (5+)
- Customer reviews (5+ minimum)

### AI Directories (Futurepedia, TAAFT)
- AI features description
- Use cases
- Pricing
- Screenshots
- Demo video

### Local Directories (Yelp, Google)
- Business name + address
- Hours of operation
- Photos (5+)
- Category selection
- Description

## Positioning Variants

### For Startup Directories
**Headline:** [Outcome statement]
**Description:** [Problem] → [Solution] → [Proof] → [CTA]

**Example:**
> Turn your slowest hours into your busiest. Example Marketplace sends Flash Offers to your regulars when you have empty hours. One tap, zero commission. $4.99/mo.

### For SaaS Directories
**Headline:** [Competitor] alternative — [Price] vs [Their Price]
**Description:** [Feature comparison] → [Price advantage] → [CTA]

**Example:**
> Square Loyalty alternative — $4.99/mo vs $45/mo. 82% feature coverage at 5-30x lower price.

### For AI Directories
**Headline:** AI-powered [capability]
**Description:** [AI feature] → [Use case] → [Benefit] → [CTA]

**Example:**
> AI-powered deal suggestions for local businesses. Example Marketplace uses AI to suggest deals based on YOUR menu, YOUR regulars, YOUR slow hours.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Submitting too early** — landing page not ready
2. **Same description everywhere** — AI penalizes duplicate content
3. **No alternative pages** — missing destination for link equity
4. **No demo video** — 2.7x fewer upvotes on Product Hunt
5. **Ignoring reviews** — G2/Capterra need 5+ reviews minimum
6. **Not tracking** — no system to monitor submissions
7. **Giving up too early** — takes 2-4 weeks for approval
