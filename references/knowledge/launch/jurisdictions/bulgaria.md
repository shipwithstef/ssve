# Bulgaria — Company Registration Guide

**Last updated:** 2026-04-28
**Staleness:** 180 days (laws change slowly)

---

## Overview

Bulgaria offers one of the EU's most startup-friendly environments:
- **Corporate tax:** 10% (flat, lowest in EU)
- **EU member:** Access to single market, GDPR compliant
- **Digital registration:** Online company formation via EU Directive 2019/1151
- **Low costs:** Registration from €25, monthly costs from €125

---

## Business Structures

### BULSTAT Freelancer (Self-Employed)
**Best for:** Solo founders, testing ideas, pre-revenue

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Founders** | 1 person |
| **Liability** | Unlimited (personal assets at risk) |
| **Capital required** | None |
| **Registration cost** | ~50-100 BGN (~€25-50) |
| **Monthly costs** | 0-50 BGN (~€0-25) |
| **Registration time** | 1-3 days |

**Pros:**
- Simplest, cheapest option
- No accounting required (simplified)
- Good for testing ideas

**Cons:**
- Unlimited liability
- Less credible for B2B
- No employee hiring

**When to use:** Pre-revenue, testing ideas, solo work

---

### ET (Едноличен търговец — Sole Trader)
**Best for:** Small businesses, single owner

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Founders** | 1 person |
| **Liability** | Unlimited (personal assets at risk) |
| **Capital required** | None |
| **Registration cost** | ~100-150 BGN (~€50-75) |
| **Monthly costs** | 50-150 BGN (~€25-75) |
| **Registration time** | 3-5 days |

**Pros:**
- Simple structure
- Full control
- Lower costs than LLC

**Cons:**
- Unlimited liability
- Harder to raise investment
- Limited growth potential

**When to use:** Small local businesses, freelancers scaling up

---

### EOOD (Еднолично дружество с ограничена отговорност — Single-member LLC)
**Best for:** Startups, solo founders wanting liability protection

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Founders** | 1 person |
| **Liability** | Limited (company assets only) |
| **Capital required** | 2 BGN minimum (~€1) |
| **Registration cost** | ~200-400 BGN (~€100-200) |
| **Monthly costs** | 250-400 BGN (~€125-200) |
| **Registration time** | 1-5 days (online via EU directive) |

**Pros:**
- Limited liability
- Single founder allowed
- Professional credibility
- EU-compatible structure

**Cons:**
- Accounting required
- Higher monthly costs
- More administrative burden

**When to use:** Startups, B2B businesses, raising investment

---

### OOD (Дружество с ограничена отговорност — LLC)
**Best for:** Businesses with multiple founders

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Founders** | 2+ people |
| **Liability** | Limited (company assets only) |
| **Capital required** | 2 BGN minimum (~€1) |
| **Registration cost** | ~200-400 BGN (~€100-200) |
| **Monthly costs** | 250-400 BGN (~€125-200) |
| **Registration time** | 1-5 days |

**Pros:**
- Multiple founders
- Limited liability
- Easier to raise investment
- Professional structure

**Cons:**

- More complex governance

**When to use:** Co-founded businesses, partnerships

## EU Directive 2019/1151 — Fast Company Formation

The EU requires member states to offer digital company registration:

| Requirement | Bulgaria Status |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Online registration** | ✅ Via Registry Agency portal |
| **Max 5 working days** | ✅ Usually 1-3 days |
| **Single point of contact** | ✅ Commercial Register handles all |
| **Digital templates** | ✅ Available on portal |
| **Digital signatures** | ✅ Accepted |

**What this means:**
- No physical visits required
- Registration fully online
- Templates pre-filled
- Fast processing (1-3 days typical)

## Registration Process (EOOD)

### Step 1: Choose Company Name
- Check availability at Commercial Register
- Must be unique in Bulgaria
- Can be in English

### Step 2: Prepare Documents
- Articles of Association (digital template available)
- Founder declaration
- Capital deposit proof

### Step 3: Deposit Capital
- Minimum 2 BGN (~€1)
- Deposit in Bulgarian bank
- Get deposit certificate

### Step 4: Register Online
- Go to https://portal.registryagency.bg
- Upload documents
- Pay fee (~100 BGN)
- Submit application

### Step 5: Get BULSTAT
- Automatic after registration
- Used for all official purposes

### Step 6: Open Bank Account
- Use registration documents
- Business account required

### Step 7: Register for Taxes
- At National Revenue Agency
- VAT registration if needed

## Taxation

### Corporate Tax
- **Rate:** 10% (flat, lowest in EU)
- **Applies to:** All profits
- **Due:** March 31 following tax year

### VAT
- **Rate:** 20% (standard), 9% (reduced)
- **Threshold:** 50,000 BGN (~€25,000) annual turnover
- **Registration:** Mandatory above threshold

### Dividend Tax
- **Rate:** 5% (withholding)
- **Applies to:** Distributed profits

### Personal Income Tax
- **Rate:** 10% (flat)
- **Applies to:** Salary, freelance income

## When to Upgrade Structure

| Stage | Recommended Structure | Why |
|-------|----------------------|-----|
| **Idea testing** | BULSTAT | Simple, cheap, fast |
| **Pre-revenue** | BULSTAT | No liability risk yet |
| **First revenue** | EOOD | Liability protection |
| **Hiring employees** | EOOD | Required for employees |
| **Raising investment** | EOOD or OOD | Investors expect LLC |
| **Multiple founders** | OOD | Required for partnerships |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not registering for VAT** — mandatory above 50,000 BGN turnover
2. **Mixing personal/business funds** — keep separate accounts
3. **Ignoring accounting requirements** — EOOD requires proper books
4. **Not filing annual reports** — penalties for late filing
5. **Forgetting social security** — mandatory contributions

## Recommendation

**For most startups:**
1. Start with BULSTAT freelancer (test idea)
2. Upgrade to EOOD when you have revenue or need liability protection
3. Consider OOD only if you have co-founders

**For international startups:**
- Bulgaria is EU member → access to single market
- 10% corporate tax is lowest in EU
- Digital registration is fast and cheap
- Good for holding companies and IP
