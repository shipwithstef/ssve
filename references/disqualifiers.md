# Disqualifiers

## Hard Vetoes (Reject Immediately)

| # | Veto | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | Requires physical presence or local market only | Violates online product mandate |
| 2 | Evidence strength < 4 | Pure speculation, no signals |
| 3 | Takes > 2 weeks to build at builder's pace | Too big for Stage 1 |
| 4 | No evidence anyone pays for similar products | No demand validation |
| 5 | Requires paid infrastructure from day 1 | Builder has $0 budget |
| 6 | Violates builder's repeated failure pattern | Same death mode as 2+ past projects |
| 7 | Requires skills builder explicitly lacks and cannot acquire | Impossible execution |
| 8 | Illegal, TOS-violating, or reputation-risky | Ethics/safety |
| 9 | Builder would never use it themselves | No founder-market fit |

## Soft Vetoes (Warn, But Allow Override)

| # | Warning | Action |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Competes directly with free Google/Facebook product | Need extreme differentiation |
| 2 | Requires network effects to work (empty room problem) | Need launch strategy for first users |
| 3 | Seasonal demand only | Flag revenue timing risk |
| 4 | Regulatory complexity (health, finance, legal) | Flag compliance burden |

When a disqualifier fires, log it in "Considered and Rejected" and move on.
