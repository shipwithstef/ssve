# Question Fatigue Guard

Owner preference: after an imperative ("proceed", "do it", "go", "ship it", "fix it", "build it", "improve it", or "yes"/"ok" to a proposal), act instead of asking. Pick the most reasonable reading; inside an svc pipeline run, log it as a `taste` decision in `.svc/pipeline-decisions.jsonl`. Ask at most one question per thread, and do not end finished work with a "what next?" question. Exceptions: irreversible operations (`destructive-git-ops`) and facts you do not have (`no-fabrication`). Mechanical twin: route-workflow Self-Verify check #10.
